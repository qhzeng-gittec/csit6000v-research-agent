Based on `doc.txt`, a digital asset previously sold as a security would be reevaluated at the time of later offers or sales, with additional considerations relating to the "efforts of others." Specifically, the reevaluation would examine:

1. **Whether the efforts of an Active Participant (AP), including any successor AP, continue to be important** to the value of an investment in the digital asset.
2. **Whether the network on which the digital asset functions operates in such a manner** that purchasers would no longer reasonably expect an AP to carry out essential managerial or entrepreneurial efforts.
3. **Whether the efforts of an AP are no longer affecting the enterprise's success.**

These considerations build on the general "reasonable expectation of profits derived from the efforts of others" analysis (the third prong of the Howey test), which is an objective inquiry focused on the economic reality of the transaction and how the digital asset is offered and sold.
