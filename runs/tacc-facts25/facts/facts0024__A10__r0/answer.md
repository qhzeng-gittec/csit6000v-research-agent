According to `doc.txt`, when evaluating whether a digital asset previously sold as a security should be reevaluated at the time of later offers or sales, there are additional considerations relating to the "efforts of others," including but not limited to:

- "Whether or not the efforts of an AP, including any successor AP, continue to be important to the value of an investment in the digital asset." (doc.txt)
- "Whether the network on which the digital asset is to function operates in such a manner that purchasers would no longer reasonably expect an AP to carry out essential managerial or entrepreneurial efforts." (doc.txt)
- "Whether the efforts of an AP are no longer affecting the enterprise's success." (doc.txt)
