According to `doc.txt`, the self-employment tax rate is 15.3 percent, distributed as follows:

- **12.4 percent** is allocated to the **Social Security** system
- **2.9 percent** goes to **Medicare**

The document also notes that if you were an employee, your employer would pay half (so only 6.2 percent for Social Security and 1.45 percent for Medicare would come out of your wages). But as a self-employed individual, you are considered both the employer and the employee, so the entire 15.3 percent burden is yours. You do, however, get a tax deduction for one-half of the self-employment taxes paid as an above-the-line deduction to arrive at adjusted gross income.
