**Who the "owner" is and his experience:**
The document doesn't mention Elon Musk at all. The person at the center of this trip is **Jared Isaacman**, a tech entrepreneur and the founder/CEO of the Shift4 credit card-processing company. According to the document, this was his **second chartered flight with SpaceX**, with two more still planned under his personally financed space exploration program named **Polaris** (after the North Star). His first spaceflight was in 2021, when he paid an undisclosed sum and took along contest winners and a pediatric cancer survivor, raising more than $250 million for St. Jude Children's Research Hospital.

**Specs of the trip (Polaris Dawn):**
- A **five-day trip** that ended with a splashdown in the Gulf of Mexico near Florida's Dry Tortugas (about 70 miles/113 km west of Key West) on Sunday, Sept. 15, 2024.
- The crew of four: Isaacman, two SpaceX engineers (Anna Menon and Scott "Kidd" Poteet), and a former Air Force Thunderbird pilot (Sarah Gillis, a classically trained violinist).
- The spacecraft hit a **peak altitude of 875 miles (1,408 kilometers)** after Tuesday's liftoff — higher than anyone has traveled since NASA's moonwalkers.
- The crew orbited nearly **460 miles (740 km) above Earth**, higher than the ISS and Hubble Space Telescope.
- They performed the **first private spacewalk** (Thursday), lasting less than two hours, with the Dragon capsule's hatch open barely a half-hour. Isaacman emerged only up to his waist to test SpaceX's new spacesuit, followed by Gillis, who was knee high.
- Isaacman became the 264th person ever to perform a spacewalk; Gillis the 265th. Until then, all spacewalks were done by professional astronauts.
- The splashdown near the Dry Tortugas was SpaceX's first time targeting that location, prompted by two weeks of poor weather forecasts.

**How the trip benefited the company:**
SpaceX considers the brief spacewalk a **starting point to test spacesuit technology for future, longer missions to Mars**. Additionally, Isaacman shared the cost of the Polaris Dawn mission with SpaceX (he won't disclose how much he spent).
