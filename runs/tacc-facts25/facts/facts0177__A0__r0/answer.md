**Why the delay from 2024 to 2025:**

The document explains that while Nintendo hasn't officially announced a follow-up to the Switch, rumors suggest the next console will arrive in 2025 rather than 2024. In February 2024, the Japanese publication Nikkei reported that the proposed Nintendo Switch 2 would launch in March 2025 at the earliest, contradicting previous reports. The article noted that the 2025 date was likely due to Nintendo stockpiling consoles to avoid scalping at launch. The document draws a parallel to the original Switch's 2017 launch, when the console suffered from inflated aftermarket pricing due to resellers. Additionally, Nintendo's president Shuntaro Furukawa confirmed that Nintendo will support the current-gen Switch until at least March 2025, which aligns with a 2025 release window for the successor.

**What it will look like:**

There isn't a lot of confirmed information on the design. However, VGC reported that development kits are already with partner studios, and sources cited that the new console could keep the portability of its predecessor and feature a cartridge slot for physical games. Interestingly, despite the OLED model being the most recent edition, a VGC report suggests the next-gen Switch may potentially feature an LCD screen instead. A 2023 patent (published in September 2023) suggests a revamped Joy-Con controller using a magnetic field for controls rather than plastic circuit boards, which could help address the notorious Joy-Con drift issue. However, the document cautions that patents don't necessarily translate directly to future products.

**Backwards compatibility:**

There's no official confirmation yet. Nintendo of America president Doug Bowser alluded to the Nintendo Account system, stating it will allow Nintendo to communicate with players "if and when we make a transition to a new platform, to help ease that process." This could suggest existing games may work on the next console, but the document emphasizes this is speculative and should be taken "with a pinch of salt." It's also unclear whether current accessories and controllers will be compatible with the new model.
