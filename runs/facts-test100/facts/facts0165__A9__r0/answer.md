

According to doc.txt, amlodipine is used alone or in combination with other medications to treat high blood pressure in adults and children 6 years and older. It is also used to treat certain types of angina (chest pain) and coronary artery disease, which the document describes as narrowing of the blood vessels that supply blood to the heart. Amlodipine belongs to a class of medications called calcium channel blockers. It lowers blood pressure by relaxing the blood vessels so the heart does not have to pump as hard, and it controls chest pain by increasing the supply of blood to the heart. The document notes that if taken regularly, amlodipine controls chest pain, but it does not stop chest pain once it starts, and that it helps control high blood pressure, angina, and coronary artery disease but does not cure these conditions.

Regarding side effects, the document states that amlodipine may cause side effects and advises telling a doctor if any of the following symptoms are severe or do not go away: swelling of the hands, feet, ankles, or lower legs; nausea; stomach pain; dizziness or lightheadedness; and muscle stiffness or tremors. It also lists some side effects that can be serious, requiring a call to the doctor immediately or emergency medical treatment: more frequent or more severe chest pain, and a rapid, pounding, or irregular heartbeat.
