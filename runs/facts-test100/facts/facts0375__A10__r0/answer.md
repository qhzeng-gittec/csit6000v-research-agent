

Based on `doc.txt`, the agreement does not grant the Customer a general right to use GEM's training materials for their own employees. What it states is:

- The Services "are only for Customer's internal use and Customer may not use the Services to supply any consulting, support or training services to any third party."
- Regarding the Materials: "Customer shall be entitled to keep and use all Materials provided by GEM to Customer, but without any other license to exercise any of the intellectual property rightstherein, all of which are hereby strictly reserved to GEM."
- The scope of use is restricted: "The use of any Materials will be limited to use by the specific personsto whom the Services are provided."
- The Materials "may not be copied electronically or otherwise whether or not for archive purposes, modified including translated, re-distributed, disclosed to third parties, lent, hired out, made available to the public, sold, offered for sale, shared, or transferred in any other way."

So the document permits the Customer to keep and use the Materials, but only by "the specific personsto whom the Services are provided," and it does not state that the Materials may be used for the Customer's employees generally.
