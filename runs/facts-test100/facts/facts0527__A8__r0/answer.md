

**Section 1 summary (one bullet per paragraph, History paragraph excluded):**

- Education is a fundamental value and a paramount state duty, requiring a uniform, safe, high-quality free public school system. "The education of children is a fundamental value of the people of the State of Florida" (doc.txt).
- Class sizes must be capped: 18 or fewer for pre-K–3, 22 or fewer for grades 4–8, 25 or fewer for grades 9–12. "does not exceed 18 students" (doc.txt).
- The state, not local districts, pays to shrink classes, funding at least two fewer students per classroom yearly from 2003-2004. "Payment of the costs associated with reducing class size to meet these requirements is the responsibility of the state and not of local schools districts" (doc.txt).
- Every four-year-old in Florida gets a voluntary, free, high-quality pre-kindergarten program. "Every four-year old child in Florida shall be provided by the State a high quality pre-kindergarten learning opportunity" (doc.txt).
- The program is organized to help each child make age-appropriate progress in language, cognitive, emotional, social, regulatory, and moral development. "designed to address and enhance each child's ability to make age appropriate progress" (doc.txt).
- Programs must start by the 2005 school year, funded with money added to existing education, health, and development programs. "shall be implemented no later than the beginning of the 2005 school year" (doc.txt).

**Importance of a pre-kindergarten program:**

The document does not use the word "importance," but it states the program's purpose: it is "designed to address and enhance each child's ability to make age appropriate progress in an appropriate range of settings in the development of language and cognitive capabilities and emotional, social, regulatory and moral capacities through education in basic skills" (doc.txt). This supports the state's broader duty "To assure that children attending public schools obtain a high quality education" (doc.txt).
