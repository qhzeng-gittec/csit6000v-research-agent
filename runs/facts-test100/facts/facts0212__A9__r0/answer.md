

According to `doc.txt`, the self-employment tax rate is 15.3 percent, distributed as follows:

- **12.4 percent** is allocated to the Social Security system
- **2.9 percent** goes to Medicare

The document also notes that as a self-employed individual, you are considered both the employer and the employee, so the entire self-employment tax burden is yours (whereas an employee would have their employer pay half, with only 6.2 percent for Social Security and 1.45 percent for Medicare taken out of wages). You do, however, get a tax deduction for one-half of the self-employment taxes paid as an above-the-line deduction to arrive at adjusted gross income.
