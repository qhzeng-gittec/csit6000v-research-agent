1. **Industrial structure biased to natural resources supply industry** — Manufacturing industry has not been developed enough: its share of total Hokkaido GDP is less than 10%, compared with more than 20% of the Japanese average. Within manufacturing, food processing dominated in 2005 and the share of assembly industry was only 12%, compared with 48% in the Japanese average.

2. **Excessive construction industry from heavy infrastructure investment** — The basic development strategy of heavy infrastructure investment developed an excessive construction industry that now requires continuous infrastructure investment, and this situation could not give good conditions for diversifying the industrial structure.

3. **Decreasing natural forestry and fishery resources** — The document states Hokkaido has faced problems of decreasing natural forestry and fishery resources, a change in basic economic and social conditions that must be considered in future development programs.

4. **Decline of the coal industry** — Since around the 1960s, the energy revolution shifted energy from coal to oil, and Hokkaido's coal industry started to decline. The last coal mine closed in 2002, ending the coal producing industry. Areas that depended on coal were greatly affected and still struggle with economic decline and collapsing communities; there are many impoverishing areas that used to depend on coal mine industry.

5. **Damage to the fishery industry** — The 200-mile economic zone system introduced internationally in 1976 greatly damaged Japanese northern sea fishery, which had been a main profit source for Hokkaido's fishery industry.

6. **Severe global competition in agriculture** — Although Hokkaido is ranked No.1 in Japan for producing many agricultural products, global competition has been getting severe and has required efforts to differentiate its products from foreign and other regions' products.

7. **Long-lasting economic recession** — Hokkaido's regional economy has been weakened, and the financial condition of many local self-governments is facing very critical conditions.
