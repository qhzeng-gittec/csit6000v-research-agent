According to `doc.txt`, the main source of income for theme parks is the **admission price**. It represents between 49 and 60 percent of overall theme park revenues (Vogel, 2010; Mintel, 2011). Other revenue sources are smaller: food and beverage accounts for about 14–17 percent, merchandise sales for 6–10 percent, games for about 4 percent, with parking fees, concession charges, and sponsorship funds making up the remainder.
