

According to `doc.txt`, a **10-free nail polish** is a non-toxic nail polish formula that omits ten specific chemicals and products. The ten excluded ingredients are:

1. **Dibutyl phthalate**
2. **THPHP (Triphenyl phosphate)**
3. **Toluene**
4. **Xylene**
5. **Ethyl tosylamide**
6. **Camphor**
7. **Formaldehyde**
8. **Formaldehyde resin**
9. **Parabens**
10. **Gluten**

The document explains that because of this substitution of toxic chemicals, 10-free polish is considered one of the safest types of nail polish. It is described as even better than 7-free polish because it excludes additional chemicals and products that are not only toxic but also environmentally damaging and unethical to use. The document also notes that the extra exclusions matter for specific health reasons — for example, parabens interfere with the hormonal system and are linked to carcinogens that can cause skin and breast cancer, triphenyl phosphate can affect hormone regulation, reproductive systems, and metabolism, and gluten can cause severe adverse reactions in people with gluten allergies, intolerances, or celiac disease.
