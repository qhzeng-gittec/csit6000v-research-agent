**What the program covers**
- It balances accounting theory with tools used in the industry and equips you with skills for entry-level accounting positions.
- You learn to complete accounting tasks from bookkeeping to preparing financial statements and personal income tax returns, and expand knowledge of business concepts including economics and finance.
- Courses incorporate accounting software to build computer literacy and up-to-date technical skills.
- In the final semester you can apply for a work placement to practise in a real work setting.

**Career outcomes**
- Prepares you for entry-level positions in financial accounting, managerial accounting, payables and receivables, and taxation.
- Typical roles: accounts payable clerk, accounts receivable clerk, bookkeeper/payroll clerk, junior staff accountant, accounting assistant.

**Success factors** — the program suits students who enjoy problem solving and critical thinking, are inquisitive and analytical, work well independently and in groups, are detail-oriented, organized and adaptable, are comfortable with computer applications, and have a high standard of ethics.

**Learning outcomes** — graduates can record financial transactions and prepare financial statements in compliance with Canadian GAAP (for sole proprietorships, partnerships, private enterprises, publicly accountable enterprises and non-profits, as applicable), apply management and financial accounting concepts to decision-making, prepare personal income tax returns and basic tax planning, analyze organizational structures and the impact of economic variables, legislation, ethics, technology and the environment, and outline internal control and risk management.

**Program of study (4 levels)**
- Level 01: ACC2201 Financial Accounting I (56h), BUS2301 Business Computer Applications (42h), ENL1813B Communications I (42h), MGT2201 Business Fundamentals (42h), QUA2210 Basic Business Mathematics (56h)
- Level 02: ACC2202 Financial Accounting II (56h), ACC2343 Spreadsheet Applications (56h), ECO2200 Economic Issues (42h), ENL1823B Communications II (42h), FIN2230 Finance (42h)
- Level 03: ACC2209 Financial Accounting III (70h), ACC2233 Management Accounting I (56h), ACC2262 Introduction to Personal Taxation (56h), ACC2385 Accounting Software Applications (56h), plus one English General Education Elective (42h)
- Level 04: ACC2211 Payroll and Compliance (56h), ACC2234 Management Accounting II (56h), ACC2265 Audit Principles and Business Issues (56h), one elective (ACC0012 Integrated Accounting Practice or ACC0044 Work Experience, 56h), plus GED0214C General Education Elective (42h)

**Fees (2023/2024)**
- Tuition and ancillary fees via the Tuition and Fees Estimator tool; more info at the Registrar's Office website; fees are subject to change.
- Books and supplies cost approximately $600–$800 per term, up to $1,000 in Levels 03 and 04; available from the campus store.

**Admission requirements (2024/2025; 2023/2024 is the same except the 2024/2025 list adds the Duolingo English Test option)**
- College eligibility: OSSD or equivalent (Basic Level senior English/Math or Workplace/Open courses trigger testing), or ACE certificate, or GED, or Mature Student status (19+ without a diploma; possible $50 academic achievement testing fee).
- Program eligibility: English Grade 12 (ENG4C or equivalent) and Mathematics Grade 12 (MAP4C or equivalent).
- International transcripts: proof of subject requirements, possible language proficiency proof; domestic applicants with international transcripts must be evaluated through ICAS or WES.
- Language proficiency: IELTS Academic overall 6.0 (min 5.5 each band), or TOEFL iBT overall 80 (min 20 each component), or (2024/2025) Duolingo DET overall 110 (min 110 Literacy, none below 95).
- If qualified applicants exceed places, selection is based on proficiency in English and mathematics.

**Application**
- Apply through ontariocolleges.ca with official transcripts; current Ontario secondary students should notify their Guidance Office.
- Fall and Winter applications received by February 1 get equal consideration; later ones are first-come, first-served.
- Registrar's Office contact details are listed (1385 Woodroffe Ave, Ottawa; 613-727-0002).

**Additional information**
- September or January start; January starters complete level 2 in Spring and continue to level 3 in Fall.
- Classes may be scheduled 8:00 AM–10:00 PM, Monday–Friday.
- Work placement is optional in level 4, Winter term only; you receive credit for ACC0044 in lieu of a fifth on-campus course. Eligibility: full-time regular on-campus registration, all level 1–3 courses completed, no academic encumbrances, certain academic standings; due to high demand, some students may need to secure their own placement with program coordinator approval.
- Degree pathways: some courses may transfer to a degree through university articulation agreements.
- CPA note: students considering a professional accounting designation should inquire with CPA Ontario; Algonquin College courses are not directly transferrable to CPA unless transferred through a recognized university articulation agreement.

**Course notes relevant to your equipment**
- ACC2262 (Personal Taxation) and ACC2385 (Accounting Software Applications) strongly recommend a Windows-based laptop (not a Mac).
- BUS2301 strongly recommends a PC; Mac students may need to install additional software.

One clarification: the document does not describe a standalone "Financial Accounting" program — it describes the Business – Accounting diploma, within which Financial Accounting I, II, and III form a three-course series (I: identify, measure, record and report financial transactions and the accounting cycle; II: expand on measuring, recording, analyzing and reporting transactions; III: corporate transactions and more complex topics, building on the first two).
