Based on `doc.txt`, prompt outside help (i.e., immediate medical attention or a professional) is needed in the following situations:

1. **Stuck ring in an emergency**: If the ring gets stuck on your finger and, in cases of emergency and/or discomfort, you can't remove it yourself, you should seek immediate medical attention (after first trying cold water and gentle soap, and holding your hand above your heart).

2. **Child swallowing the ring**: Seek immediate medical attention if you know or suspect that a child has swallowed the ring.

3. **Persistent skin irritation**: If you experience redness or skin irritation on your finger due to the ring, remove it immediately; if symptoms persist longer than 2–3 days of not using the Oura product, contact a dermatologist.

4. **Medical conditions or changes to sleep/activity**: You should always consult a physician if you have questions about a medical condition or changes you intend to make to your sleep or activity based on Oura Services, and never disregard or delay seeking professional medical advice because of something you've read from Oura Services. In particular, if you feel unexpected, repeated, or long-term pain, fatigue, or discomfort due to changes to your sleep or activity, consult a physician before continuing.
