

According to `doc.txt`, the study examined weight changes among 4.24 million US adults (ages 18–84) from January 2019 through May 2021 to determine whether weight gain increased during the pandemic. Its outcome was:

- Compared with the pre-pandemic weight trend, there was a small increase of **0.1 kg** in weight during the first year of the pandemic (March 2020 through March 2021).
- Weight changes during the pandemic varied by sex, age, and initial BMI, but the **largest mean increase across these characteristics was less than 1.3 kg**.
- Weight increases were generally greatest among **women, adults with a BMI of 30 or 35 kg/m², and younger adults**.
