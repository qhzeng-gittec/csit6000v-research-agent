**Safety context from the document:** In the first 6 months (when SIDS risk is highest), the safest place for a baby to sleep is on their back in their own sleep space — a bedside crib lets you keep baby close without bed-sharing, which carries risks of suffocation and overheating. The Lullaby Trust advises the baby sleep on their own surface, in the same room, for at least the first 6 months.

**Things to check before choosing (per the document):**
- Whether it works with your bed frame (divan beds may need longer straps or foldable legs)
- Bed height (most have adjustable heights, but check measurements)
- Mattress must be firm, flat and breathable
- How easy the drop-down side is to operate (ideally one-handed, quietly)
- Ease of assembly and cleaning, portability, and extra features (rocking, tilt, detachable bassinet)

So, for example, if you want the easiest drop-down side, the document's pick is the **Chicco Next2Me Magic**; if you want a portable bassinet you can move around the house, the **SnuzPod 4**; and if budget matters most, the **Red Kite Cozysleep** at £84.99.
