**Origination and Amendments**
- **December 12, 2019:** The company entered into a credit agreement with various financial institutions providing for a **$350.0 million term loan** (the "2019 Term Loan"), alongside a $25.0 million revolving credit facility (with a $15.0 million letter of credit sub-facility).
- **December 11, 2020:** The credit agreement was amended (the "2020 Credit Agreement") to **increase the 2019 Term Loan to $550.0 million** (the "2020 Term Loan") and to **extend the maturity date** of the 2019 Term Loan and the Revolving Credit Facility to **December 11, 2025**.
- **June 15, 2023:** The 2020 Credit Agreement was further amended (the "Credit Agreement") to **increase the total size of the 2020 Term Loan to $650.0 million** (the "Term Loan") upon the closing of the Google Domains Asset Acquisition. Effective June 30, 2023, **LIBOR was replaced with SOFR** as the benchmark rate.

**Use of Proceeds**
- 2019 Term Loan: repurchase, and subsequent retirement, of outstanding capital stock.
- 2020 Term Loan: a dividend on all outstanding capital stock.
- The additional $100.0 million under the Term Loan: partially funded the Google Domains Asset Acquisition, together with cash on hand.

**Interest Rate**
- Prior to June 30, 2023: at the company's option, LIBOR or the bank's alternative base rate (ABR), in each case plus an applicable margin.
- Effective June 30, 2023: SOFR replaced LIBOR as the benchmark. The ABR is the greater of the prime rate, the federal funds effective rate plus the applicable margin, or the SOFR quoted rate plus the applicable margin.
- The applicable margin is based on an indebtedness-to-consolidated EBITDA ratio and ranges from **1.25% to 2.25% on SOFR loans** and **0.25% to 1.25% on ABR loans**.

**Outstanding Balance and Repayment**
- As of **June 30, 2024, $546.9 million was outstanding** under the Term Loan.
- The Term Loan requires **scheduled quarterly principal payments** in aggregate annual amounts equal to **7.50% of the principal for 2023 and 2024, and 10.00% for 2025**, with the balance due at maturity.
- The Credit Agreement includes customary prepayment requirements triggered by events such as asset sales, incurrence of indebtedness, and sale-leasebacks.

**Covenants and Security**
- Negative covenants limit (subject to negotiated exceptions) incurring additional indebtedness or issuing preferred stock, incurring liens, entering into M&A agreements, disposing of assets, and paying dividends/distributions.
- A financial covenant requires an **indebtedness-to-consolidated EBITDA ratio of no more than 3.75** (subject to customary equity cure rights), with a 0.50 step-up available up to two times for a material permitted acquisition (not elected as of June 30, 2024).
- The obligations are **guaranteed by the company's wholly-owned domestic subsidiaries** and **secured by substantially all of the guarantors' assets**, subject to certain exceptions.
- As of June 30, 2024, the company was in compliance with all applicable covenants, including the Financial Covenant.
