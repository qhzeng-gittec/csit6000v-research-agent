

Based on `doc.txt` (the GEM Training Services Agreement), the answer is **no — not for their employees generally**. The agreement restricts use of the training materials in two key ways:

1. **Section 4.1 (Intellectual Property):** While the Customer "shall be entitled to keep and use all Materials provided by GEM to Customer," the agreement states that "The use of any Materials will be limited to use by the specific persons to whom the Services are provided." The Customer receives no other license to the intellectual property rights in the Materials, and the Materials may not be copied (electronically or otherwise), modified, re-distributed, disclosed to third parties, lent, shared, or transferred in any other way.

2. **Section 1 (Services):** The Services are "only for Customer's internal use," and the Customer may not use them to supply consulting, support, or training services to any third party.

So the Customer may keep and use the Materials, but only the specific individuals who actually received GEM's training services may use them — the Customer may not extend use of the Materials to other employees of their own.
