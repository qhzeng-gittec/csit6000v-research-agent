

Based on `doc.txt` (section 3.2.1–3.2.5), the document describes five types of nanoparticles:

**1. Zinc oxide (ZnO) NPs**
- Benefits: (a) boost insulin signaling by enhancing insulin receptor phosphorylation and phosphoinositide 3-kinase activity; (b) repair pancreatic tissue damaged by diabetes, improving blood sugar and serum insulin levels; (c) act as antioxidants and reduce inflammation, with antidiabetic activity in animal models that often surpasses other treatments.
- Best for: **Type 2 diabetes** — studies comparing ZnO NPs with standard antidiabetic drugs like Vildagliptin show effectiveness in treating type 2 diabetes.

**2. Magnesium (MgO) NPs**
- Benefits: (a) reduce blood sugar levels; (b) improve insulin sensitivity; (c) regulate lipid levels, and when delivered via the DPAP system to diabetic target cells, reduce sugar oxidation.
- Best for: **Type II diabetes** — the document states MgO NPs "may be a promising treatment for type II diabetes."

**3. Cerium oxide (CeO2) NPs**
- Benefits: (a) serve as a regenerative agent that prevents nerve damage caused by diabetes and treats diabetic neuropathy; (b) may help reduce complications from gestational diabetes; (c) show potential in treating oxidative disorders and brain injuries.
- Best for: **Diabetic neuropathy and gestational diabetes** (the document notes further research is needed to validate these findings).

**4. Copper (Cu) NPs**
- Benefits: (a) superior antioxidant properties; (b) ability to inhibit alpha-amylase and alpha-glucosidase; (c) prevent cardiovascular defects by enhancing nitric oxide availability in the vascular endothelium and reducing oxidative stress, and aid wound healing in diabetic mice (accelerating recovery and controlling bacterial infections).
- Best for: **Type 2 diabetes** — explicitly stated as effective in treating Type 2 diabetes.

**5. Selenium (Se) NPs**
- Benefits: (a) less toxic, with antioxidant properties that scavenge peroxides and protect cellular macromolecules; (b) preserve pancreatic β-cells, boost insulin secretion, and reduce glucose levels; (c) enhance liver function and lower inflammatory markers.
- Best for: **T2DM (type 2 diabetes mellitus)** — also noted as a treatment for diabetes and insulin resistance.
