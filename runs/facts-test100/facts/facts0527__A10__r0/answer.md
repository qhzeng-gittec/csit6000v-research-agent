**Section 1 summary (one bullet per paragraph, history paragraph excluded):**

- The state must provide a uniform, efficient, safe, secure, high-quality free public school system, with class sizes capped at 18 (pre-K–3), 22 (4–8), and 25 (9–12) students per teacher.
- Every four-year-old in Florida must be offered a voluntary, free, high-quality pre-kindergarten program delivered according to professionally accepted standards.
- The program must start no later than the 2005 school year, funded with money added to existing education, health, and development programs.

**Importance of the pre-kindergarten program:**

According to the document, the program matters because it is "designed to address and enhance each child's ability to make age appropriate progress ... in the development of language and cognitive capabilities and emotional, social, regulatory and moral capacities through education in basic skills" (doc.txt). In other words, its importance is that it helps each child develop language, thinking, and emotional, social, and moral abilities at an age-appropriate level.
