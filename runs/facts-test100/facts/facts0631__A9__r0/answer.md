According to `doc.txt`, the Standard-series (Gen 5) logical CPUs are based on the following processors:

- Intel E5-2673 v4 (Broadwell) 2.3 GHz
- Intel SP8160 (Skylake)
- Intel Xeon Platinum 8272CL 2.5 GHz (Cascade Lake)
- Intel(R) Xeon Scalable 2.8 GHz processor (Ice Lake)

The document also notes that in the standard-series (Gen 5), 1 vCore = 1 hyper thread.
