1. **Expand presence** in existing western Washington market areas, leveraging community-bank advantages (timely decisions, personalized service, access to senior managers) amid bank consolidation.
2. **Diversify the portfolio** — limiting speculative construction and land development loans in favor of lower-risk owner/builder, custom construction, multi-family, commercial business, and commercial real estate loans, and cross-selling products.
