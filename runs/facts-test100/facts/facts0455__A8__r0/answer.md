**Overall trend**
- "Data from multiple sources suggest that mental health symptoms and substance use have increased since the beginning of the COVID-19 pandemic." (doc.txt)
- "most of the 2020 data suggest an increase in behavioral health morbidity in the United States over the course of the COVID-19 pandemic." (doc.txt)

**Stress and mental health symptoms**
- A SHADAC survey in April 2020 "found that over 90% of U.S. adults reported experiencing additional levels of stress caused by the COVID-19 pandemic." (doc.txt)
- A Kaiser Family Foundation survey found that "In March 2020, 32% of respondents felt that worry or stress related to coronavirus had a negative impact on their mental health. In April 2020 that number rose to 45%, and in July 2020, 53% reported that pandemic-related stress was affecting their mental health." (doc.txt)
- NCHS data indicate "the percentage of Americans experiencing symptoms of a mental health disorder appears to have increased during the COVID19 pandemic." (doc.txt)
- CDC analysis of emergency department visits showed "socioeconomic and psychosocial-related visits increased during April 2020 (compared with April 2019), while total ED visits decreased over 40%." (doc.txt)

**Suicide**
- "approximately twice as many U.S. adults reported serious consideration of suicide in the previous 30 days compared with 2018 (10.7% versus4.3%)." (doc.txt)
- The Disaster Distress Helpline "experienced a 335% increase in calls during the first five months of the pandemic." (doc.txt)
- However, "it appears that suicide mortality has decreased compared with previous years," and "An increase in suicidal thoughts does not necessarily equate to an increase in suicide attempts or suicide deaths." (doc.txt)

**Substance use and overdoses**
- ODMAP "reported an increase of 11% in fatal overdoses and a 19% increase in nonfatal overdoses from March through May 2020 compared with the same months in 2019." (doc.txt)
- CDC data showed "the rate of overdose deaths accelerating after the pandemic began," with the rise "particularly notable for deaths involving synthetic opioids." (doc.txt)
- "the ODMAP and CDC data suggest that drug-related overdoses and overdose deaths have increased during the COVID-19 pandemic." (doc.txt)

**Vulnerability of people with substance use disorders**
- "Individuals with substance use disorders may be at higher risk of contracting SARS-CoV-2 due to unstable housing situations, high incarceration rates, or the inability to physically distance themselves." (doc.txt)
- "those with substance use disorders may be at higher risk for complications of COVID-19 because substance use can often suppress the immune system or inhibit respiratory functioning." (doc.txt)

The document also notes data limitations: "Comprehensive national data for 2020 are not yet available," and ODMAP submissions "appeared to be trending upward prior to the onset of the pandemic, making it difficult to determine the effects of the pandemic and mitigation measures using these data."
