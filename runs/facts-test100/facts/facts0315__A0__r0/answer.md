## The three that matter for you

**1. BMW i4 — the efficiency king.** At 120 MPGe combined (122 city / 119 highway), it's the most efficient vehicle in the document by a wide margin — 15 MPGe better than the next-best (i5) and 30 better than the i7. It's a four-door fastback with an 80.7-kWh battery. Caveats: range is 276 miles, and if you step up to the dual-motor M50 (0–60 in 3.3 s), range drops to 271 miles.

**2. BMW i7 — the range king.** 321 miles per charge is the longest range in the document, with 90 MPGe combined. It's the flagship luxury sedan — plush, ultramodern, quick, and quiet. One important caveat from the doc: the optional 20- or 21-inch wheels reduce both range and efficiency, so to hit the 321-mile/90-MPGe figures you'd have to skip those wheels.

**3. BMW i5 — the best balance.** The range-optimized eDrive40 pairs the second-best efficiency (105 MPGe combined, with a very even 104 city / 105 highway) with the third-best range (295 miles). It's the electric version of the 5-series with a cabin drawn from the larger i7. The performance M60 xDrive (510 hp) is available if you want more power, though the doc doesn't list its separate efficiency/range figures.

## My recommendation

Since money is no object and you care about **both** efficiency and range, I'd buy the **BMW i5 eDrive40**: it's the only vehicle that is top-three in *both* categories (105 MPGe and 295 miles), so you don't have to sacrifice much on either axis.

- If **efficiency is the single most important thing**, take the **i4** instead — its 120 MPGe is unmatched in the document, and 276 miles is still respectable.
- If **maximum range is the single most important thing** (e.g., long trips between chargers), take the **i7** — 321 miles is 45 miles more than the i4 and 26 more than the Lyriq (314 mi) — just avoid the big optional wheels.

The other vehicles fall short on at least one of your two criteria: the Lyriq (314 mi but only 88 MPGe), iX (307 mi, 83 MPGe), Blazer EV (279 mi, 96 MPGe), and the Audis (all 265 miles or less, with the e-tron GT at just 249 miles and 85 MPGe).
