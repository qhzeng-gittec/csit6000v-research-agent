Specifically, the document says the reevaluation would consider, including but not limited to:

1. **Whether the efforts of an Active Participant (AP), including any successor AP, continue to be important** to the value of an investment in the digital asset.
2. **Whether the network on which the digital asset is to function operates in such a manner** that purchasers would no longer reasonably expect an AP to carry out essential managerial or entrepreneurial efforts.
3. **Whether the efforts of an AP are no longer affecting the enterprise's success.**

In other words, the reevaluation turns on whether the AP's (or a successor AP's) managerial/entrepreneurial efforts remain essential to the asset's value and the enterprise's success, or whether the network has become sufficiently decentralized that purchasers no longer reasonably expect such efforts.
