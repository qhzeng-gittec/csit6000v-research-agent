According to `doc.txt`, the self-employment tax rate is 15.3 percent, distributed as follows:

- **12.4 percent** is "allocated to the Social Security system" (doc.txt)
- **2.9 percent** is "going to Medicare" (doc.txt)

The document also notes that as an employee, "your employer would pay half, which means only 6.2 percent would be taken out of your wages for Social Security and 1.45 percent for Medicare" (doc.txt), but "as a self-employed individual, you are considered both the employer and the employee, which makes the entire selfemployment tax burden yours" (doc.txt). You "get a tax deduction for one-half of the self-employed taxes paid as an abovethe-line deduction to arrive at adjusted gross income" (doc.txt).
