

Based solely on `doc.txt` (a collection of customer reviews of a Hisense TV and its Alexa skill), here are the pros and cons:

## Pros
- **Works well for some users**: Tania A. (5★) says it "works excellent" and she "love[s] it"; Ernie (5★) says it's "working great."
- **Easy setup and reliable Alexa control for some**: Jared B. (5★) found it "easy to setup and works great" — turning the TV on/off, changing volume, and muting/unmuting all worked on the first command.
- **Works on first attempt for some models**: Eileen G. (5★) reports it works on the first attempt with the Hisense 55U7G (Android TV).
- **Functional commands**: Ricardo (3★) says the app works 100% with Alexa for on/off and volume; Amazon Customer (3★) notes commands have been added over time (on/off, volume, channel, inputs); Juanito D. (3★) can turn on/off, modify volume, and activate apps.
- **Acceptable short-term value**: Tracy L. (2★) says the TV "works fine for a year or two"; Tammy B. (1★) concedes "the price was good."

## Cons
- **Hardware reliability**: Michael M. (1★) reports HDMI ports failed within a year with no reason; Bunny S. (1★) had sound cut out after a month or two, requiring power cycles, and the TV was eventually deemed unrepairable; JP (1★) had a TV that stopped turning on after 2 years.
- **Poor customer service/warranty**: Michael M. says Hisense denied warranty over a "microscopic pinhole" in the screen; Bunny S. calls it the "worst customer service ever" (no-show repair appointment, can't reach a supervisor, refund still pending); JP was hung up on, left on hold, and told the warranty would not be honored.
- **Clunky, confusing interface**: Michael M. describes the UI as "non-nonsensical, clunky, and awkward," with a "maze of sub-menu[s]," menus that "don't make sense," and cryptic one-word settings with no detail; Tammy B. calls it the "worst TV to try and figure out how to navigate."
- **Weak auto-volume leveling**: Michael M. says it "fluctuates wildly in volume compared to Vizio."
- **App problems**: Tracy L. says apps "won't work right or not at all," pre-installed apps can't be uninstalled, and the TV "won't update at all" — calling it a "throw away TV."
- **Frustrating Alexa setup requiring workarounds**: John S. (3★) had to enable the skill, create a Hisense account, log out, re-enable the skill, and log back in — "Stupid it can't just work the first time"; Cole (5★) had to sign out of the Hisense account and sign back in; Ernie warns you must use the same Google/Alexa account or the device won't appear; Juanito D. notes it's "a little hard to set up."
- **Missing/limited commands**: Matt (3★) says it "doesn't start any apps" (only on/off, input, volume) and requires enabling "Wake-on-Lan/Wifi" in network settings; Amazon Customer (3★) notes commands to open specific apps are still missing; Juanito D. couldn't change input via command (Alexa couldn't find a device that could do it).
- **Voice assistant failures for some**: Tammy B. says she "couldn't get Alexa or The voice Assistant to work on it ever."
