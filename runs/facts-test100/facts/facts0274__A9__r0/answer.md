Based on `doc.txt`, three reasons iPSCs are a better approach than ESCs for treating diabetes are:

1. **Ethical considerations**: Ethical concerns associated with ESCs have restricted their widespread clinical application, whereas iPSCs possess similar pluripotent characteristics to ESCs, thereby addressing those ethical considerations.

2. **Autologous origin / reduced rejection risk**: iPSCs offer the advantage of being autologous — they originate from the individual's own cells — which minimizes the risk of immunological reactions or rejection when transplanted tissues derived from iPSCs are used.

3. **Unlimited replicative capacity and pluripotency**: Due to their unlimited replicative capacity (self-renewal) and pluripotency, iPSCs offer a promising avenue for differentiating into pancreatic endocrine lineage cells, specifically functional insulin-producing pancreatic β cells.
