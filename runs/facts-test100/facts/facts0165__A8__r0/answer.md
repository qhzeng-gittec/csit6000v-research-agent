

According to doc.txt, amlodipine is prescribed for several purposes. "Amlodipine is used alone or in combination with other medications to treat high blood pressure in adults and children 6 years and older." (doc.txt) It "is also used to treat certain types of angina (chest pain) and coronary artery disease (narrowing of the blood vessels that supply blood to the heart)." (doc.txt) The document explains how it works: "Amlodipine is in a class of medications called calcium channel blockers. It lowers blood pressure by relaxing the blood vessels so the heart does not have to pump as hard. It controls chest pain by increasing the supply of blood to the heart." (doc.txt) The document also notes that "If taken regularly, amlodipine controls chest pain, but it does not stop chest pain once it starts," (doc.txt) and that "Amlodipine helps to control high blood pressure, angina, and coronary artery disease, but does not cure these conditions." (doc.txt)
