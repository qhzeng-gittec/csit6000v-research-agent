According to `doc.txt`, the main source of income for theme parks is the admission price. Revenues are generated from a variety of sources, but primarily from the admission price, which represents between 49 and 60 percent of overall theme park revenues (Vogel, 2010; Mintel, 2011). Other sources include food and beverage (about 14–17 percent), merchandise sales (6–10 percent), games (about 4 percent), and the remainder from parking fees, concession charges, and sponsorship funds.
