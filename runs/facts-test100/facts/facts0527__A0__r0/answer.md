

**Section 1 summary (each bullet ≤30 words):**

- Florida must provide a high-quality free public school system and cap class sizes by 2010: 18 for preK–3, 22 for grades 4–8, and 25 for grades 9–12. (28 words)
- The state shall provide every 4-year-old a voluntary, free, high-quality pre-kindergarten program that fosters age-appropriate language, cognitive, emotional, social, regulatory, and moral development. (23 words)
- These programs must begin by the 2005 school year, funded with new funds in addition to those for existing education, health, and development programs. (27 words)

**Importance of the pre-kindergarten program (per the document only):**

According to the document, the program matters because it is "designed to address and enhance each child's ability to make age appropriate progress" in developing language and cognitive capabilities and emotional, social, regulatory, and moral capacities, through education in basic skills and other skills the Legislature may deem appropriate. In other words, its importance lies in helping every 4-year-old develop these foundational abilities—offered voluntarily, free of charge, at high quality, and delivered according to professionally accepted standards.
