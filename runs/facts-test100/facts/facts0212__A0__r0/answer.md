

According to `doc.txt`, the self-employment tax rate is **15.3 percent**, distributed as follows:

- **12.4 percent** is allocated to the **Social Security** system
- **2.9 percent** goes to **Medicare**

The document also notes that as a self-employed individual, you are considered both employer and employee, so the entire tax burden is yours (unlike an employee, whose employer would pay half). However, you can deduct one-half of the self-employment taxes paid as an above-the-line deduction to arrive at adjusted gross income.
