

Based on `doc.txt` (a collection of customer reviews of a Hisense TV and its Alexa skill), the pros and cons are:

**Pros**
- Several 5-star reviewers say it "works excellent," "working great," and "works, first attempt" (Tania A., Ernie, Eileen G.).
- Jared B. (5 stars): "Easy to setup and works great" — with Alexa, turning the TV on/off, changing volume, and muting/unmuting "all worked on the first command."
- Ricardo (3 stars): "The app works 100% with Alexa" — turning the TV on/off and controlling volume.
- Amazon Customer (3 stars): over time, commands were added to turn the TV on/off, adjust volume, and change channel and inputs.
- Juanito D. (3 stars): after enabling "Wake on Wireless Network" in Network settings, can turn the TV on/off, modify volume, and activate apps.
- Cole (5 stars): after signing out of the Hisense account and signing back in, "it works great."

**Cons**
- Reliability/durability complaints: Michael M. (1 star) says an HDMI port failed within a year and the TV should not be bought; Bunny S. (1 star) says the sound started going out after a month or two and the TV was declared unrepairable; JP (1 star) says the TV stopped turning on after 2 years; Tracy L. (2 stars) says it works fine for a year or so, then the apps won't work right or at all, won't update, and pre-installed apps can't be uninstalled — calling it "a throw away TV."
- Customer service/warranty complaints: Michael M. says Hisense deemed the failure non-warranty because of a microscopic pinhole in the screen; Bunny S. calls it the "worst customer service ever," with a no-show repair appointment and no access to a supervisor; JP says they were hung up on, left on hold, and told they would not honor the warranty.
- User interface complaints: Michael M. calls the interface "non-nonsensical, clunky, and awkward," with a maze of sub-menus, menus that don't make sense, and cryptic one-word setting descriptions; the auto-volume leveling "still fluctuates wildly in volume." Tammy B. (1 star) calls it "the worst TV to try and figure out how to navigate" and says Alexa/voice assistant never worked on it.
- Alexa skill setup is a pain: John S. (3 stars) had to enable the skill, create a Hisense account, log out, re-enable the skill, and log back in — "Stupid it cant just work the first time." Matt (3 stars) says "it's a pain to setup" and you must change "Wake-on-Lan/Wifi" to ON in network settings. Ernie (5 stars) warns that if you don't set everything up with the same Google or Alexa account, you won't see the device.
- Missing functionality: Matt (3 stars) says it "doesn't start any apps — just on and off, change input, and control volume"; Amazon Customer (3 stars) notes it's "still missing commands to open up specific apps"; Juanito D. (3 stars) says he can't change the input with commands — Alexa says it can't find any device that can do that.
