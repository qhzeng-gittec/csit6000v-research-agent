**Origination and amendments**
- On December 12, 2019, the company entered into a credit agreement with various financial institutions providing for a $350.0 million term loan (the "2019 Term Loan") and a $25.0 million revolving credit facility (which included a $15.0 million letter of credit sub-facility).
- On December 11, 2020, the credit agreement was amended (the "2020 Credit Agreement") to increase the 2019 Term Loan to $550.0 million (the "2020 Term Loan") and to extend the maturity date for the 2019 Term Loan and the Revolving Credit Facility to December 11, 2025.
- On June 15, 2023, the 2020 Credit Agreement was further amended (the "Credit Agreement") to increase the total size of the 2020 Term Loan to $650.0 million (the "Term Loan") upon the closing of the Google Domains Asset Acquisition, and, effective June 30, 2023, replaced LIBOR as the benchmark rate with SOFR.

**Use of proceeds**
- The 2019 Term Loan borrowings were used to provide for the repurchase, and subsequent retirement, of outstanding capital stock.
- The 2020 Term Loan borrowings were used to provide for a dividend on all outstanding capital stock.
- The additional $100.0 million of borrowings under the Term Loan were used to partially fund the Google Domains Asset Acquisition, together with cash on hand.

**Interest rate**
- Borrowings under the 2020 Credit Agreement were subject to an interest rate equal to, at the company's option, LIBOR or the bank's alternative base rate (the "ABR"), in either case plus an applicable margin, prior to June 30, 2023. Effective June 30, 2023, LIBOR was replaced with SOFR.
- The ABR is the greater of the prime rate, the federal funds effective rate plus the applicable margin, or the SOFR quoted rate plus the applicable margin.
- The applicable margin is based on an indebtedness to consolidated EBITDA ratio as prescribed under the Credit Agreement and ranges from 1.25% to 2.25% on applicable SOFR loans and 0.25% to 1.25% on ABR loans.

**Outstanding balance and repayment**
- As of June 30, 2024, $546.9 million was outstanding under the Term Loan.
- The Term Loan requires scheduled quarterly principal payments in aggregate annual amounts equal to 7.50% for 2023 and 2024, and 10.00% for 2025, in each case on the Term Loan principal amount, with the balance due at maturity.
- The Credit Agreement includes certain customary prepayment requirements for the Term Loan, triggered by events such as asset sales, incurrence of indebtedness, and sale leasebacks.

**Security and covenants (as they relate to the Term Loan)**
- The obligations under the Credit Agreement are guaranteed by the company's wholly-owned domestic subsidiaries and are secured by substantially all of the assets of the guarantors, subject to certain exceptions.
- The Credit Agreement contains customary affirmative covenants and events of default, and negative covenants including limitations (subject to negotiated exceptions) on incurring additional indebtedness or issuing preferred stock, incurring liens, entering into merger and acquisition agreements, disposing of assets, and paying dividends and distributions.
- The company is required to maintain an indebtedness to consolidated EBITDA ratio of not more than 3.75 (the "Financial Covenant"), subject to customary equity cure rights, with a 0.50 step-up in the event of a material permitted acquisition (electable up to two times during the life of the facility; as of June 30, 2024, the company had not elected to implement this step-up).
- As of June 30, 2024, the company was in compliance with all applicable covenants, including the Financial Covenant.
