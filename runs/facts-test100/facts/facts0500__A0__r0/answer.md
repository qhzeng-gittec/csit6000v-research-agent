

Based on the "Excellent Life for Cows" section of `doc.txt`, the company's measures to improve cows' quality of life include:

- **Relying on independent third-party standards** to advance animal care throughout its dairy supply chain.
- **Auditing farms** in 2021 and 2022 against two standards: the company's own Caring Dairy Standard and the Global Animal Partnership (GAP) Dairy care standard.
- **Using audit findings to drive improvements** — the audits pinpointed specific opportunities to improve cow care (while also revealing industry-level barriers that can slow the wider adoption of higher-level certifications).
- **Encouraging continuous improvement among farm partners**, with about 20% of them achieving GAP certification after going through the rigorous audits — a step above standard industry performance.
