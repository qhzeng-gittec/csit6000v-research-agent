

**Section 1 summary (History paragraph excluded):**

- Florida values education and must provide free, high-quality public schools, with class-size limits of 18 (pre-K–3), 22 (4–8), and 25 (9–12) students, funded by the state.
- Every four-year-old in Florida gets a voluntary, free, high-quality pre-kindergarten program, delivered to professionally accepted standards.
- These programs must start by the 2005 school year, using new funds added to those for existing education, health, and development programs.
