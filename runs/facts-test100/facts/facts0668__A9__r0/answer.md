

According to `doc.txt`, a 10-free nail polish is a formula that omits ten chemicals and products: dibutyl phthalate, TPHP (triphenyl phosphate), toluene, xylene, ethyl tosylamide, camphor, formaldehyde, formaldehyde resin, parabens, and gluten.

The document adds that:

- Because it substitutes out these toxic chemicals, this formula is considered one of the safest types of nail polish.
- It is "even better" than 7-free polish because it excludes additional chemicals and products that are not only toxic but also environmentally damaging and unethical to use.
- The three extras beyond the 7-free list are:
  - **Parabens** – preservatives used to aid longevity that interfere with the hormonal system and are linked to well-researched carcinogens that can cause skin and breast cancer.
  - **Triphenyl phosphate (TPHP)** – a toxin used to aid the malleability of the varnish; ongoing or extended exposure can cause changes in hormone regulation, affecting reproductive systems and metabolism.
  - **Gluten** – which for those with a gluten allergy or intolerance can lead to severe adverse reactions, and for those with celiac disease, topical exposure to wheat products can cause irritation.
