

Based on `doc.txt`, the challenges to Hokkaido's economy described in the article are:

1. **Industrial structure biased toward natural resources; weak manufacturing** — "from the view point of industrial structure, it is still biased to natural resources supply industry. Manufacturing industry has not been developed enough yet." The article notes that "share of manufacturing industries in the total Hokkaido GDP is less than 10%, compared with more than 20% of Japanese average," and that in 2005 "food processing industry dominated, and share of assembly industry is only 12%, compared with 48% in Japanese average."

2. **Over-reliance on infrastructure investment** — "It has developed excessive construction industry and they have come to require continuous infrastructure investment. And this situation could not give a good condition for diversifying its industrial structure."

3. **Decreasing natural resources and impoverished former coal areas** — "Now Hokkaido has faced with the problems decreasing natural forestry and fishery resources. And there are many impoverishing areas which used to depend on coal mine industry."

4. **Damage to the fishery industry** — "200 mile economic zone system was introduced internationally in 1976. After that, Japanese northern sea fishery, which was used to be main profit sources for Hokkaido fishery industry, was damaged greatly."

5. **Decline of the coal industry** — "since around 1960's, energy revolution progressed rapidly and the shift from coal to oil as energy source happened. Then the important industrial sector, coal industry in Hokkaido started to decline." The last coal mine closed in 2002, and "Some areas, where depended on coal industry were greatly affected by closing mines and still have struggled with their economic decline and collapsing communities."

6. **Severe global competition in agriculture** — "Even such situation, global competition has been getting severe and has required the efforts to differentiate them from foreign and other regions' products."

7. **Economic recession and strained local finances** — "under the long lasted economic recession, Hokkaido's regional economy has been weakened and financial condition of many local self-governments is facing with very critical conditions."
