## Program Overview
- **Type/Length:** Ontario College Diploma, 2 years, offered at the **Ottawa Campus** (Algonquin College).
- **Program Code:** 0214C01FWO
- **Purpose:** Balances accounting theory with industry tools and equips you with essential skills for entry-level accounting positions — bookkeeping, preparing financial statements, and personal income tax returns, plus business concepts like economics and finance.
- **Start dates:** September or January. January starters must complete level 2 in the Spring term and continue into level 3 in the Fall term.
- **Schedule:** Classes may be scheduled between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM, Monday–Friday.

## Program of Study (4 levels)
- **Level 01:** ACC2201 Financial Accounting I (56h), BUS2301 Business Computer Applications (42h), ENL1813B Communications I (42h), MGT2201 Business Fundamentals (42h), QUA2210 Basic Business Mathematics (56h)
- **Level 02:** ACC2202 Financial Accounting II (56h), ACC2343 Spreadsheet Applications (56h), ECO2200 Economic Issues (42h), ENL1823B Communications II (42h), FIN2230 Finance (42h)
- **Level 03:** ACC2209 Financial Accounting III (70h), ACC2233 Management Accounting I (56h), ACC2262 Introduction to Personal Taxation (56h), ACC2385 Accounting Software Applications (56h), plus one English General Education elective (e.g., ENL1725, ENL1726, ENL1798, ENL1825, ENL1829 — 42h)
- **Level 04:** ACC2211 Payroll and Compliance (56h), ACC2234 Management Accounting II (56h), ACC2265 Audit Principles and Business Issues (56h), plus one elective — ACC0012 Integrated Accounting Practice (56h) or ACC0044 Work Experience (56h) — and one General Education elective (GED0214C, 42h)

## Work Placement
- Optional, available only in **Level 4, Winter term only**. You receive credit for ACC0044 in lieu of a fifth on-campus course.
- **Eligibility:** registered full-time in the regular on-campus program, all level 1–3 courses completed, no academic encumbrances, and certain academic standings. Due to high demand, some students may need to secure their own placement (subject to coordinator approval).

## Learning Outcomes (highlights)
- Record financial transactions in compliance with Canadian GAAP (sole proprietorships, partnerships, private and publicly accountable enterprises, non-profits)
- Prepare/present financial statements and reports per Canadian GAAP
- Apply management accounting and financial management concepts to decision-making
- Prepare individuals' income tax returns and basic tax planning
- Analyze organizational structures, economic variables, legislation, ethics, technology, and internal controls/risk management

## Employment
- Prepares you for entry-level positions in **financial accounting, managerial accounting, payables/receivables, and taxation**.
- Typical roles: accounts payable clerk, accounts receivable clerk, bookkeeper/payroll clerk, junior staff accountant, accounting assistant.

## Success Factors
The program suits students who enjoy problem solving and critical thinking, are inquisitive/analytical, work well independently and in groups, are detail-oriented, organized, adaptable, comfortable with computer applications, and have a high standard of ethics.

## Admission Requirements (2024/2025)
- **College eligibility:** OSSD or equivalent (Basic-level senior English/Math or Workplace/Open courses may trigger testing), or ACE certificate, or GED, or Mature Student status (19+, no diploma; possible $50 academic achievement testing fee).
- **Program eligibility:** Grade 12 English (ENG4C or equivalent) and Grade 12 Mathematics (MAP4C or equivalent).
- **International transcripts:** proof of subject requirements; domestic applicants with international transcripts must be evaluated through ICAS or WES.
- **Language proficiency (if required):** IELTS Academic overall 6.0 (min 5.5 per band); or TOEFL iBT overall 80 (min 20 each component); or Duolingo DET overall 110 (min 110 Literacy, none below 95).
- If qualified applicants exceed places, selection is based on English and mathematics proficiency. Academic Upgrading may help if you don't meet requirements.

## Application
- Apply through ontariocolleges.ca (60 Corporate Court, Guelph, ON; 1-888-892-2228) with official transcripts.
- Fall/Winter applications received by **February 1** get equal consideration; after that, first-come, first-served.
- Registrar's Office: 1385 Woodroffe Ave, Ottawa, ON; 613-727-0002 / 1-800-565-4723.

## Fees & Expenses
- Tuition/ancillary fees via the Tuition and Fees Estimator (algonquincollege.com/fee-estimator); fees subject to change.
- Books/supplies: approximately **$600–$800 per term**, up to **$1,000** in Levels 03 and 04. Available from the campus store.

## Important Notes
- **CPA designation:** If you're considering a professional accounting designation, contact **CPA Ontario** before enrolling. Algonquin College courses are **not directly transferrable to CPA** unless transferred through a recognized university articulation agreement.
- **Degree pathways:** Some courses may transfer toward a degree through university articulation agreements.
- **Technology:** A **Windows-based laptop (not a Mac)** is strongly recommended for ACC2262 (tax software) and ACC2385 (accounting software); BUS2301 also recommends a PC.
