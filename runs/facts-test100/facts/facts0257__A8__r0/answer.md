

Based on `doc.txt`, a new device starts in its default settings: standard 'right-handed' mode, the lowest cursor speed, standard 'right-click' mode, and the buzzer on. Counting the beeps for each requested change:

1. **Right-handed operation**: No beeps. A new unit is already in the standard mode — "One beep indicates the unit is set to standard 'right-handed' mode, whereas two beeps indicates 'left-handed' operation" (doc.txt). Since it is already right-handed, no button press is needed (pressing the left button would switch it to left-handed).

2. **Cursor speed to 2**: 2 beeps. Starting from the lowest speed, one brief press of the middle button is needed, and "Each time you do so, the unit emits a number of beeps, between 1 and 4. One beep indicates the lowest speed and 4 the highest" (doc.txt) — so speed 2 gives 2 beeps.

3. **Double click**: 2 beeps. One brief press of the right button changes it from right-click to Double-Click, and "two beeps indicates 'Double-Click' operation" (doc.txt).

4. **Buzzer off**: 2 beeps. "To achieve this, press and hold the right button while in Learn Mode, until two long beeps are heard" (doc.txt). Note the beeps are still audible because "The buzzer is now disabled, although it will still operate while in Learn Mode" (doc.txt).

**Total: 0 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 beeps.**
