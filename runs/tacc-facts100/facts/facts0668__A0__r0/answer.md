

According to `doc.txt`, a **10-free** nail polish is a non-toxic formula that omits ten specific chemicals/ingredients that may have damaging effects on the user. The ten excluded substances are:

1. **Dibutyl phthalate**
2. **THPHP (Triphenyl phosphate)**
3. **Toluene**
4. **Xylene**
5. **Ethyl tosylamide**
6. **Camphor**
7. **Formaldehyde**
8. **Formaldehyde resin**
9. **Parabens**
10. **Gluten**

The document explains that because of this substitution of toxic chemicals, 10-free polish is considered one of the safest types of nail polish. It is better than 7-free polish because it excludes additional chemicals and products that are not only toxic but also environmentally damaging and unethical to use. For example, parabens interfere with the hormonal system and are linked to carcinogens that can cause skin and breast cancer; triphenyl phosphate can cause changes in hormone regulation affecting reproductive systems and metabolism; and gluten can cause severe adverse reactions in people with gluten allergies, intolerances, or celiac disease.
