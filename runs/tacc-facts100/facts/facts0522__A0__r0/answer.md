1. **When the ring gets stuck on your finger** — after trying the suggested remedies (wetting the finger with cold water and gentle soap and slowly twisting the ring, or holding your hand above your heart until blood pressure drops), the document says: *"In cases of emergency and/or discomfort, when you can't remove the ring yourself, seek immediate medical attention."*

So, per the document, prompt outside (medical) help is needed when you cannot remove a stuck ring yourself due to emergency or discomfort, or when a child is known or suspected to have swallowed the ring.
