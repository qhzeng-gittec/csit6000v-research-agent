**Origination and amendments**
- **December 12, 2019:** The company entered into a credit agreement with various financial institutions providing for a **$350.0 million term loan** (the "2019 Term Loan"), alongside a $25.0 million revolving credit facility (with a $15.0 million letter of credit sub-facility).
- **December 11, 2020:** The credit agreement was amended (the "2020 Credit Agreement") to **increase the 2019 Term Loan to $550.0 million** (the "2020 Term Loan") and to **extend the maturity date** of the 2019 Term Loan and the Revolving Credit Facility to **December 11, 2025**.
- **June 15, 2023:** The 2020 Credit Agreement was further amended (the "Credit Agreement") to **increase the total size of the 2020 Term Loan to $650.0 million** (the "Term Loan") upon the closing of the Google Domains Asset Acquisition. Effective **June 30, 2023**, LIBOR was replaced with **SOFR** as the benchmark rate.

**Use of proceeds**
- 2019 Term Loan borrowings: repurchase and subsequent retirement of outstanding capital stock.
- 2020 Term Loan borrowings: a dividend on all outstanding capital stock.
- The additional $100.0 million under the Term Loan: partially funded the Google Domains Asset Acquisition, together with cash on hand.

**Interest rate**
- Prior to June 30, 2023: at the company's option, LIBOR or the bank's alternative base rate (ABR), plus an applicable margin.
- Effective June 30, 2023: SOFR replaced LIBOR as the benchmark.
- ABR is the greater of the prime rate, the federal funds effective rate plus the applicable margin, or the SOFR quoted rate plus the applicable margin.
- The applicable margin is based on an indebtedness-to-consolidated EBITDA ratio and ranges from **1.25% to 2.25% on SOFR loans** and **0.25% to 1.25% on ABR loans**.

**Outstanding balance and repayment**
- As of **June 30, 2024, $546.9 million was outstanding** under the Term Loan.
- Scheduled **quarterly principal payments** in aggregate annual amounts equal to **7.50% of the Term Loan principal for 2023 and 2024, and 10.00% for 2025**, with the balance due at maturity.
- Customary prepayment requirements are triggered by events such as asset sales, incurrence of indebtedness, and sale-leasebacks.

**Covenants and security**
- The company must maintain an **indebtedness-to-consolidated EBITDA ratio of no more than 3.75** (the "Financial Covenant") for the fiscal quarter ended June 30, 2024 and each quarter thereafter, subject to customary equity cure rights. The covenant is subject to a **0.50 step-up** upon a material permitted acquisition (electable up to two times); as of June 30, 2024, no step-up had been elected.
- As of June 30, 2024, the company was in compliance with all applicable covenants, including the Financial Covenant.
- Obligations are **guaranteed by wholly-owned domestic subsidiaries** and **secured by substantially all of the guarantors' assets**, subject to certain exceptions.

**Interest expense**
- Total interest expense related to the indebtedness was **$10.1 million and $20.5 million** for the three and six months ended June 30, 2024, respectively, and **$8.6 million and $16.7 million** for the three and six months ended June 30, 2023, respectively.
