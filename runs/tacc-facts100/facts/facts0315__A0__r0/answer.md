## My recommendation: the BMW i4 (eDrive40)

Since money is no object and **both** efficiency and range are critical, the i4 is the only vehicle in the document that leads on efficiency while still delivering 300+ miles of range:

- **Best efficiency in the entire list:** 120 MPGe combined (122 city / 119 highway) — a full 17 MPGe ahead of the next-best (Q4 e-tron at 103).
- **Range:** The mid-level eDrive40 gets "more than 300 miles on a full charge, according to the EPA" (the listed 276-mile figure is the base spec; the M50 drops to 271 miles).
- **Caveat:** If you want the racy M50 (0–60 in 3.3 s), you sacrifice range — 271 miles — so for your priorities, stick with the eDrive40.

## The one alternative worth considering: BMW i7

If **range is the single most important number**, the i7 wins outright at **321 miles per charge** — the longest in the document. But it's the least efficient of the top contenders at 90 MPGe combined, and the doc warns that the optional 20- or 21-inch wheels reduce both range and efficiency, so you'd need to avoid those to keep the 321-mile figure. It's also the priciest option here ($106,695) — though you said money is no object.

## Why not the others

- **Cadillac Lyriq (314 mi, 88 MPGe)** and **BMW iX (307 mi, 83 MPGe)**: good range, but both are 20–30+ MPGe less efficient than the i4.
- **BMW i5 (295 mi, 105 MPGe)**: solid all-rounder, but beaten by the i4 on both efficiency and range.
- **Audi Q8 e-tron Sportback (up to 296 mi)**: decent range, but only 81 MPGe combined (the SQ8 is worse at 73 MPGe).
- **Audi Q4 e-tron (265 mi, 103 MPGe)**: second-best efficiency, but the shortest range among the efficient options, and the AWD Sportback is 8 MPGe less efficient.
- **Audi e-tron GT (249 mi, 85 MPGe)** and **Chevy Blazer EV (279 mi, 96 MPGe)**: weakest on your two priority metrics.

**Bottom line:** Buy the **BMW i4 eDrive40** — it's the document's efficiency champion (120 MPGe) with 300+ miles of EPA range, the best combination of your two critical criteria. Choose the **i7** instead only if maximum range (321 mi) matters more to you than efficiency.
