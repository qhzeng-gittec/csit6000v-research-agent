

Based on `doc.txt`, the President's FY2024 budget request recommended a package of program-integrity legislative changes. The provisions that would create **new requirements for states** are:

1. **Data-matching requirement** – The legislation would codify the requirement for states to data match with the National Directory of New Hires (NDNH, administered by HHS) and the Prisoner Update Processing System (PUPS, administered by the Social Security Administration), to help ensure UI benefits are correctly paid to eligible individuals in a timely manner. (The document notes that currently there is no statutory requirement for states to use NDNH or several other related data cross-matches.)

2. **Disclosure to DOL-OIG** – States would be required to disclose information to the DOL Office of the Inspector General (DOL-OIG) to streamline DOL-OIG's ability to conduct audits and investigations in the UI program. This includes authorizing DOL-OIG to have direct access to the Interstate Connection Network (ICON), used for electronic transmission of interstate claims, and to the Integrity Data Hub (IDH) system, used for cross-matching UI claimants against other databases to prevent and detect fraud and improper payments.

3. **Use of penalty and interest collections** – States would be required to use penalty and interest collections solely for UI administration.

The same package also included related provisions that were authorizations rather than requirements: allowing the DOL Secretary to require a portion of a state's administrative grant to be used to correct failing performance and/or require participation in DOL technical assistance; authorizing states to retain up to 5% of recovered fraudulent UI overpayments for program integrity; providing states authority to issue a formal warning when claimants do not clearly meet work-search requirements; and allowing states to use contract support in recovery efforts under the Treasury Offset Program (TOP).
