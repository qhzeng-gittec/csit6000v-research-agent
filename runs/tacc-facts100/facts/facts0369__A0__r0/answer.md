The document states that "Revenues for the theme park industry are generated from a variety of sources, but primarily from the admission price," which represents between 49 and 60 percent of overall theme park revenues (Vogel, 2010; Mintel, 2011).

Other, smaller revenue sources include:
- Food and beverage: approximately 14–17% of revenues
- Merchandise sales (souvenirs, sundries, etc.): 6–10%
- Games: about 4%
- The remainder comes from parking fees, concession charges, and sponsorship funds from external advertisers.
