

No. According to the Training Services Agreement in `doc.txt`, the customer's use of GEM's training materials is restricted to the specific individuals who actually receive the training services:

- "The use of any Materials will be limited to use by the specific personsto whom the Services are provided." (doc.txt)

The agreement also states that the customer may keep and use the materials but holds no broader license: "Customer shall be entitled to keep and use all Materials provided by GEM to Customer, but without any other license to exercise any of the intellectual property rightstherein, all of which are hereby strictly reserved to GEM." (doc.txt)

And the materials may not be shared or copied: "In particular and without limitation, Materials may not be copied electronically or otherwise whether or not for archive purposes, modified including translated, re-distributed, disclosed to third parties, lent, hired out, made available to the public, sold, offered for sale, shared, or transferred in any other way." (doc.txt)

So while the services themselves are "only for Customer's internal use" (doc.txt), the training materials cannot be used by the customer's other employees beyond the specific persons to whom the Services are provided.
