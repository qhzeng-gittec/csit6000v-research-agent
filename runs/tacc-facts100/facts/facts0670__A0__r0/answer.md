

Based solely on `doc.txt` (a collection of customer reviews of a Hisense TV and its Alexa skill), here are the pros and cons:

## Pros
- **Works well for some users**: Tania A. (5★) says it "works excellent" and "i love it"; Ernie (5★) says it's "working great."
- **Easy setup and reliable voice control (for some)**: Jared B. (5★) found it "easy to setup and works great" — turning on/off, volume, and mute all worked on the first command with Alexa.
- **Functional after troubleshooting**: Cole (5★) reports it "works great" after signing out of the Hisense account and back in; Eileen G. (5★) says it "works, first attempt" on her Hisense 55U7G (Android TV).
- **Good command support over time**: Ricardo (3★) says the app "works 100% with Alexa" for on/off and volume; Amazon Customer (3★) notes commands for on/off, volume, channel, and inputs have been added over time; Juanito D. (3★) can turn on/off, adjust volume, and activate apps after enabling "Wake on Wireless Network."

## Cons
- **Hardware reliability issues**: Michael M. (1★) says HDMI ports failed within a year; Bunny S. (1★) had sound cut out after a month or two and was told the TV "cannot be repaired"; JP (1★) had a TV that "just stopped turning on" after 2 years; Tracy L. (2★) says it works fine for a year or two, then apps stop working and it won't update — calling it a "throw away TV."
- **Warranty and customer service problems**: Michael M.'s warranty was denied over a "microscopic pinhole in the screen"; Bunny S. describes "worst customer service ever" with a no-show repair appointment, no supervisor access, and a delayed refund; JP was hung up on, left on hold, and told the warranty would not be honored.
- **Clunky, confusing interface**: Michael M. calls the UI "nonsensical, clunky, and awkward," with menus that "don't make sense" and cryptic one-word settings; auto-volume leveling "fluctuates wildly." Tammy B. (1★) calls it "the worst TV to try and figure out how to navigate."
- **Setup difficulties with the Alexa skill**: John S. (3★) had to enable the skill, create an account, log out, and re-enable it; Matt (3★) says "it's a pain to setup" and required changing the "Wake-on-Lan/Wifi" setting; Juanito D. (3★) found setup "a little hard."
- **Limited functionality**: Matt (3★) notes it "doesn't start any apps" (only on/off, input, volume); Amazon Customer (3★) says commands to open specific apps are still missing; Juanito D. (3★) couldn't change inputs via commands; Tammy B. (1★) says Alexa/voice assistant "couldn't... work on it ever."

In short, the reviews are sharply divided: satisfied users praise easy setup and reliable voice control, while dissatisfied users cite hardware failures, poor warranty/customer service, a confusing interface, and a fiddly, incomplete Alexa integration.
