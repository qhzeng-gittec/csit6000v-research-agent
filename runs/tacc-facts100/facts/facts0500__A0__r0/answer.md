- **Relying on independent third-party standards** to advance animal care throughout its supply chain.
- **Auditing farms in 2021 and 2022** against two standards: the company's own Caring Dairy Standard and the Global Animal Partnership (GAP) Dairy care standard.
- **Using audit findings to identify specific opportunities** for improving cow care (the audits also surfaced industry-level barriers that could slow wider adoption of higher-level certifications).
- **Encouraging continuous improvement among farm partners**, with about 20% of them becoming GAP certified after going through the rigorous audits — a step the document describes as notably above standard industry performance.
