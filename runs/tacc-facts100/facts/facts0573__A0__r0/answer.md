

Based on `doc.txt`, five types of nanoparticles are described:

**1. Zinc oxide (ZnO) NPs**
- Benefits:
  1. Boost insulin signaling by enhancing insulin receptor phosphorylation and phosphoinositide 3-kinase activity.
  2. Repair pancreatic tissue damaged by diabetes, improving blood sugar and serum insulin levels.
  3. Act as antioxidants and reduce inflammation, making them candidates for treating diabetes and its related complications.
- Best for: **Type 2 diabetes** — studies comparing ZnO NPs with the standard drug Vildagliptin show they are effective in treating type 2 diabetes.

**2. Magnesium (MgO) NPs**
- Benefits:
  1. Reduce blood sugar levels in diabetic mice.
  2. Improve insulin sensitivity.
  3. Regulate lipid levels; when delivered via the polymer-directed aptamer (DPAP) system to diabetic target cells, they reduce sugar oxidation.
- Best for: **Type II diabetes** — the document suggests MgO NPs may be a promising treatment for type II diabetes.

**3. Cerium oxide (CeO2) NPs**
- Benefits:
  1. Serve as a regenerative agent, preventing nerve damage caused by diabetes and treating diabetic neuropathy.
  2. May help reduce complications from gestational diabetes (though further research is needed).
  3. Show potential in treating oxidative disorders and brain injuries.
- Best for: **Gestational diabetes** (reducing its complications) and diabetic neuropathy.

**4. Copper (Cu) NPs**
- Benefits:
  1. Superior antioxidant properties.
  2. Inhibit alpha-amylase and alpha-glucosidase.
  3. Prevent cardiovascular defects in diabetic individuals by enhancing nitric oxide availability in the vascular endothelium and reducing oxidative stress; they also aid wound healing in diabetic mice, accelerating recovery and controlling bacterial infections.
- Best for: **Type 2 diabetes** — described as effective in treating Type 2 diabetes.

**5. Selenium (Se) NPs**
- Benefits:
  1. Less toxic, with antioxidant properties that scavenge peroxides and protect cellular macromolecules.
  2. Preserve pancreatic β-cells, boost insulin secretion, and reduce glucose levels.
  3. Enhance liver function and lower inflammatory markers, helping maintain the oxidative/antioxidant balance.
- Best for: **Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM)** and insulin resistance, mitigating related complications.
