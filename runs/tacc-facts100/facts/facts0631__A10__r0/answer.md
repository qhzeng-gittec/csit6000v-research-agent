

According to `doc.txt`, the 5th gen Standard Series CPUs are based on four processor types:

"Standard-series (Gen 5) logical CPUs are based on Intel E5-2673 v4 (Broadwell) 2.3 GHz, Intel SP8160 (Skylake), Intel Xeon Platinum 8272CL 2.5 GHz (Cascade Lake) and Intel(R) Xeon Scalable 2.8 GHz processor (Ice Lake) processors." (doc.txt)

The document also notes that "In the standard-series (Gen 5), 1 vCore = 1 hyper thread." (doc.txt)
