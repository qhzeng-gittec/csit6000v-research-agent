

**Section 1 summary (one bullet per paragraph, history excluded):**

- Education is a fundamental Florida value; the state must provide a uniform, efficient, safe, secure, high-quality free public school system for all children.
- The legislature must ensure enough classrooms so class sizes stay at or below 18 (pre-K–3), 22 (grades 4–8), and 25 (grades 9–12).
- The state, not local districts, pays to reduce class sizes; the legislature must fund cuts of at least two students per classroom per year.
- Every four-year-old in Florida must be offered a voluntary, free, high-quality pre-kindergarten program delivered according to professionally accepted standards.
- The program is designed to help each child make age-appropriate progress in language, cognitive, emotional, social, regulatory, and moral development through basic skills.
- The program must begin no later than the 2005 school year, funded with money added to existing education, health, and development programs.

**Importance of the pre-kindergarten program:**

According to the document, the program matters because it is organized specifically to address and enhance each child's ability to make age-appropriate progress in developing language and cognitive capabilities, as well as emotional, social, regulatory, and moral capacities, through education in basic skills (and other skills the Legislature may determine). It is guaranteed to be voluntary, high quality, free, and delivered according to professionally accepted standards, ensuring every four-year-old in Florida receives this early development opportunity.
