

Based on `doc.txt` (the GEM Training Services Agreement), the answer is **yes, but only with strict limitations**:

1. **Internal use is permitted** – Section 1 states that the "Services are only for Customer's internal use," so using the training for the Customer's own employees is within the scope of the agreement (as long as the Customer does not use the Services to supply consulting, support, or training to any third party).

2. **Use is limited to specific persons** – Section 4.1 grants the Customer the right to "keep and use all Materials provided by GEM to Customer," but "The use of any Materials will be limited to use by the specific persons to whom the Services are provided." So the materials cannot be used by employees beyond those who actually received the training services.

3. **No copying, redistribution, or other rights** – Section 4.1 also states the Customer has "no other license to exercise any of the intellectual property rights therein," and the Materials "may not be copied electronically or otherwise... modified including translated, re-distributed, disclosed to third parties, lent, hired out, made available to the public, sold, offered for sale, shared, or transferred in any other way." All intellectual property rights remain solely with GEM.

In short: the client may use GEM's training materials internally for the specific employees who are provided the Services, but may not copy, modify, share, or redistribute them to anyone else.
