## Program Basics
- **Ontario College Diploma**, 2 years, **Program Code 0214C01FWO**, offered at the **Ottawa Campus** (Algonquin College).
- Balances accounting theory with industry tools, preparing you for entry-level positions in **financial accounting, managerial accounting, payables/receivables, and taxation**.
- You'll learn bookkeeping, preparing financial statements and personal income tax returns, plus business concepts (economics, finance), and sharpen communication, math, and technology skills using accounting software.
- **Typical graduate roles:** accounts payable clerk, accounts receivable clerk, bookkeeper/payroll clerk, junior staff accountant, accounting assistant.

## Program of Study (4 levels)
- **Level 01:** ACC2201 Financial Accounting I (56h), BUS2301 Business Computer Applications (42h), ENL1813B Communications I (42h), MGT2201 Business Fundamentals (42h), QUA2210 Basic Business Mathematics (56h)
- **Level 02:** ACC2202 Financial Accounting II (56h), ACC2343 Spreadsheet Applications (56h), ECO2200 Economic Issues (42h), ENL1823B Communications II (42h), FIN2230 Finance (42h)
- **Level 03:** ACC2209 Financial Accounting III (70h), ACC2233 Management Accounting I (56h), ACC2262 Introduction to Personal Taxation (56h), ACC2385 Accounting Software Applications (56h), plus 1 English General Education Elective (42h)
- **Level 04:** ACC2211 Payroll and Compliance (56h), ACC2234 Management Accounting II (56h), ACC2265 Audit Principles and Business Issues (56h), 1 elective — ACC0012 Integrated Accounting Practice **or** ACC0044 Work Experience (56h) — plus 1 General Education Elective (42h)

## Work Placement (optional)
- Available in **Level 4, Winter term only**; you receive credit for ACC0044 in lieu of a fifth on-campus course.
- **Eligibility:** registered full-time in the regular on-campus program, all Level 1–3 courses completed, no academic encumbrances, and certain academic standings. Due to high demand, some students may need to secure their own placement (subject to coordinator approval).

## Key Notes
- **CPA designation:** If you're considering a professional accounting credential, contact **CPA Ontario** first — Algonquin courses are **not directly transferrable to CPA** unless transferred through a recognized university articulation agreement.
- **Degree pathways:** Some courses may transfer toward a degree via university articulation agreements.
- **Schedule:** September or January start (January starters complete Level 2 in Spring, then Level 3 in Fall). Classes may run **8:00 AM–10:00 PM, Monday–Friday**.
- **Equipment:** A **Windows-based laptop (not a Mac)** is strongly recommended for ACC2262 (Personal Taxation) and ACC2385 (Accounting Software); a PC is recommended for BUS2301.
- **Costs:** Tuition via the college's fee estimator (fees subject to change). Books/supplies ≈ **$600–$800 per term**, up to **$1,000** in Levels 03–04.

## Admission Requirements (2024/2025)
- **College eligibility:** OSSD or equivalent (Basic-level senior English/Math or Workplace/Open courses may require testing), or ACE, GED, or Mature Student status (19+, $50 testing fee).
- **Program eligibility:** Grade 12 English (ENG4C or equivalent) and Grade 12 Mathematics (MAP4C or equivalent).
- **International transcripts:** proof of subject requirements; evaluated through ICAS or WES; language proficiency via IELTS (6.0 overall, min 5.5 per band), TOEFL iBT (80 overall, min 20 per component), or Duolingo DET (110 overall, 110 Literacy, none below 95).
- If qualified applicants exceed available places, selection is based on **English and mathematics proficiency**.
- Apply with official transcripts through **ontariocolleges.ca**; Fall/Winter applications received by **February 1** get equal consideration, after that first-come, first-served.

## Success Factors
The program suits students who enjoy problem solving and critical thinking, are inquisitive and analytical, work well independently and in groups, are detail-oriented, organized, adaptable, comfortable with computer applications, and possess a high standard of ethics.

## Learning Outcomes (highlights)
Graduates can record financial transactions and prepare financial statements in compliance with **Canadian GAAP** (for sole proprietorships, partnerships, private and publicly accountable enterprises, and non-profits), apply management and financial accounting concepts to decision-making, prepare personal income tax returns, analyze organizational structures and economic/legislative/ethical/technological impacts, and outline internal control and risk management systems.
