

According to `doc.txt`, the self-employment tax rate is 15.3 percent, split between two systems:

- "12.4 percent allocated to the Social Security system and the other 2.9 percent going to Medicare" (doc.txt)

The document also explains how this compares to being an employee: "If you worked as an employee of a company, your employer would pay half, which means only 6.2 percent would be taken out of your wages for Social Security and 1.45 percent for Medicare." (doc.txt)

For a self-employed person, the full burden applies: "as a self-employed individual, you are considered both the employer and the employee, which makes the entire selfemployment tax burden yours." (doc.txt) However, "You do, however, get a tax deduction for one-half of the self-employed taxes paid as an abovethe-line deduction to arrive at adjusted gross income." (doc.txt)
