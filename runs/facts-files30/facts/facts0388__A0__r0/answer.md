Based on `doc.txt` (Section 4.14, "Fraud"), if a meter seal has been broken by someone other than SRP's personnel and SRP has disconnected service, the customer must do the following before service can be restored:

1. **Pay all amounts due to SRP.** Under Section 4.14.4, SRP will not restore service to a customer disconnected for a violation of the Rules and Regulations until all amounts due have been paid. This includes:
   - The full cost or expense incurred by SRP for the removal and reinstallation of the meter(s), apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral (4.14.4).
   - Charges for periods of unmetered service, estimated from available records, and, if meters or Service Equipment were damaged, the estimated energy usage not previously billed plus SRP's costs of restoring proper metering or service (4.14.3).
   - A reconnection fee that SRP may assess to the customer's billing (4.14.1), along with any other charges SRP deems appropriate for the violation.

2. **Bring the service entrance into compliance.** The customer's service entrance must comply with SRP's then-current Electric Service Specifications before it can be re-energized (4.14.4).

In short: the customer must pay all outstanding amounts (including removal/reinstallation costs, estimated unmetered usage, and any reconnection or violation charges) and ensure the service entrance meets SRP's current Electric Service Specifications before service can be reconnected.
