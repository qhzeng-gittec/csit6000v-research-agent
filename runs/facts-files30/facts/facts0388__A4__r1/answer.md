<answer>
According to doc.txt, a broken meter seal (broken by someone other than SRP's personnel) is treated under the Fraud section (4.14), and SRP may assess a reconnection fee to the Customer's billing [E1]. If SRP has disconnected service because of a violation of the Rules and Regulations, SRP will not restore service until: (1) all amounts due SRP have been paid, which will include the full cost or expense incurred by SRP for the removal and reinstallation of the meter or meters, apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral; and (2) the Customer's service entrance complies with SRP's then-current Electric Service Specifications before it can be re-energized [E2].
</answer>
