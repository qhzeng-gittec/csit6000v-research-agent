<answer>
Section 2015 of AWIA (as described in doc.txt) makes revisions to the DWSRF (Drinking Water State Revolving Fund) provisions of SDWA, and every part of it concerns drinking water:

- **Section 2015(a)**: Amends SDWA to expressly state that DWSRF funds can be used for projects to replace or rehabilitate aging treatment, storage, or distribution systems; these projects had been eligible under EPA guidance if needed to protect public health, and this category accounts for 66.1% of the estimated drinking water infrastructure need [E1].
- **Section 2015(c)**: Revises DWSRF provisions for financial assistance to disadvantaged communities, increasing the portion of a state's capitalization grant usable for loan subsidization from 30% to 35% (with a conditional requirement to use at least 6% for these subsidies) and extending the loan amortization period from 30 to 40 years [E2].
- **Section 2015(d)**: Extends the repayment and amortization period for all DWSRF-financed projects, moving the initial principal and interest payment from one year to 18 months after project completion and authorizing extension of the amortization period from 20 to 30 years [E3].
- **Section 2015(e)**: Requires EPA to evaluate and include the cost to replace lead service lines in the drinking water infrastructure needs survey, which EPA uses to allot the DWSRF appropriation among states [E4].
- **Section 2015(f)**: Removes a fiscal year limitation, authorizing states to use a portion of their capitalization grant to fund source water assessments or update an existing one [E6].
- **Section 2015(g)**: Requires EPA to gather specified information on DWSRF administration from state drinking water administrators and report to Congress on best practices for implementing the DWSRF to facilitate the application process and improve DWSRF financial management and sustainability [E5].
</answer>
