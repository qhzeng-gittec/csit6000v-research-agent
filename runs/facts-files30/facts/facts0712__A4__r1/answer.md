**Types of sippy cups (excluding soft spout, which your daughter already uses):**

1. **Hard spout** – Encourages the transition from sucking to tilting and sipping; often best introduced after the child has mastered the soft spout [E1].
2. **Straw** – Uses a straw rather than a spout; some feel a straw is preferable for speech development, and it helps the child get used to drinking from a straw and a cup [E2].
3. **No spout / flat lid (360 cup)** – Spoutless with a flat top; liquid flows from all edges of the opening to mimic a real cup while still using a lid, and they typically lack no-spill valves [E3].
