

Here are the document's instructions as easily executable bullet points:

**Adventure Screen (Week 1, Day 1)**
- Note that the Adventure Screen is where you'll spend most of your time — "This is where you will spend a great deal of time while playing Heroes III." (doc.txt) The main window gives a close view around your heroes and cities, while the world map (upper-right corner) shows the entire world.
- Understand that most of the world map is black because "until you send a hero to explore an area you won't know what is there" (doc.txt).
- Ignore the buttons below the World Map for now, since they "will be described in greater detail later."
- Confirm your first hero, Lord Haart, is selected — "he's sitting on his horse, waiting for instructions" (doc.txt).

**Send Lord Haart to the town**
- Place your mouse cursor over the entrance to the town (between the two flag poles).
- Watch for the horse icon to rear up on its hind legs — "this means that by traveling to that location, your hero will interact with whatever is there" (doc.txt).
- Note the location's name and short description in the Rollover Bar at the bottom of the screen.
- Click on the location; green arrows show the path your hero will take to the large green X — "It is this X that marks your hero's destination." (doc.txt)
- Click again on the same location (or simply double-click the destination) to send the hero to the castle — "the first click selects the path, the second click sends the hero" (doc.txt).
- When the hero arrives at the town entrance, the view changes to the Town Screen.

**Town Screen (Castle town type)**
- Note that "there are eight different town types in Heroes III, each with its own unique creatures and buildings" (doc.txt).
- Observe that structures/upgrades you build appear in the town view, and that specific town info (town income per day and troop production per week) is in the bottom-left corner.
- Note the two rows of boxes to the right of the town info: the top row is for troops in the town's garrison, the bottom row is for troops visiting a town with a hero.

**Build a Fort**
- Click on the larger of the two buildings on the left side of the town view — the Town Hall — to enter the Hall Screen.
- In the Hall Screen, note the color coding: "Any building you can currently build is shown with its name in a green bar." (doc.txt) Red bar = can be built later, gray bar = disabled or can never be built, gold = completely built/upgraded.
- Click on the picture of the Fort to see its description and required resources.
- Click the Build button (lower left corner of the popup window) to construct the Fort.

**View the Castle Window**
- Click on the Fort to open the Castle Window, which shows information about all seven units this town can produce.
- Note that already-constructed Creature Generators show a number of Available units (currently the Guardhouse, Archers' Tower, and Barracks).
- Click the Exit button (lower right) to return to the Town Screen.

**Buy a spell book at the Mage Guild**
- Click on the round tower on the right side of the screen (your Mage Guild).
- Choose to buy a spell book: "Since Lord Haart is a Knight, he does not begin with a spell book." (doc.txt) Also, "All heroes must have a spell book before they can learn or cast spells" (doc.txt).
- Once you agree to purchase, you're shown the Mage Guild Screen, where each offered spell is listed on scrolls on the shelves.
- Left or right click on any spell scroll to see its description.
- Click the Exit button (lower right) when ready to leave.

**Recruit troops**
- Note that your hero's starting troops are "really just a token force" (doc.txt), so you should buy any available troops and add them to his army.
- Click on the large central tower (along the hill at the back of your town) to open the Recruit Pikemen window.
- Use the scroll bar to select how many troops to purchase (total cost is calculated to the right), or use the Maximum button (the four up arrows at the bottom of the popup) to automatically select the maximum number you can afford.
- Click the Maximum button, then click the Recruit button (bottom center).
- Confirm that Pikemen now appear in the garrison row of your town.
