- "Don't smoke. Cigarettes are expensive and the money adds up quickly." (doc.txt)
- "When eating out, look for coupons or special deals- many restaurants offer them! Also, order water. Drinks are highly overpriced." (doc.txt)
- "There are hundreds of free activities on campus. Join clubs, attend student concerts, or go to church-sponsored events for cheap fun." (doc.txt)
- "Don't purchase a book unless you think you really want to keep it. You can check out books for free at libraries." (doc.txt)
- "Rent movies with a group of friends or go to second-run theaters for $1 or $2 a ticket." (doc.txt)

**Food and Basic Needs**
- "Sometimes the cheaper product works just as well as the expensive one." (doc.txt)
- "Ask for generic medications at the pharmacy." (doc.txt)
- "Buying in bulk is usually a good option, but try to shop for items by the per unit price." (doc.txt)
- "Scout out garage/yard sales for housewares, furniture, and stuff to decorate your college dorm or apartment." (doc.txt)
- "Take advantage of sales by buying holiday and birthday gifts throughout the year." (doc.txt)
- "Use mail-in rebates or coupons for groceries or health and beauty items." (doc.txt)
- "Don't buy bottled water. Buy a water filtration pitcher." (doc.txt)
- "Don't buy something just because it is one sale. Consider it's a need for you before buying." (doc.txt)
- "Make home cooked meals. A home cooked stead dinner is often cheaper than a fast food binge." (doc.txt)
- "Pack a lunch instead of eating out." (doc.txt)

**Clothing**
- "Buy clothes at the end of the season when they're on sales." (doc.txt)
- "If you don't wear certain clothes anymore, take them to a consignment shop or sell them online." (doc.txt)
- "If you buy more than one of something, like 2 or 3 shirts, always ask for a discount." (doc.txt)
- "Invest in durable clothes, shoes, etc. rather than buying many cheap pairs." (doc.txt)

**Budgeting/Spending Plan**
- "Set goals for your spending and saving." (doc.txt)
- "Keep track of your spending to avoid overspent. There are apps for that!" (doc.txt)
- "Don't use a credit card if it will lead you to make more purchases! On average, people have credit cards spend 34% more." (doc.txt)
- "Before going out to spend, set a limit for yourself and stick to it!" (doc.txt)
- "Wait at least two hours before making a big purchase to be sure it's something you really need." (doc.txt)

**Transportation**

- "Keep your tires inflated properly- you'll get better gas mileage." (doc.txt)
- "Get good grades. Insurance companies offer low rates to student with 3.0+ GPA." (doc.txt)
- "Carpool with friend!" (doc.txt)
- "Drive an older car- the insurance payments and taxes will be less." (doc.txt)
- "Walk, bike, or ride to school- it's good for you to saves on gas." (doc.txt)
- "Look around for cheapest gas price before filling up. There are apps for that!" (doc.txt)

**Savings**
- "Only use ATM's of your bank. Other bank's ATM fees add up!" (doc.txt)
- "Always put part of our paycheck into a savings account." (doc.txt)
- "Spare change adds up! Get a piggy bank or change jar and don't underestimate the value of your spare changes." (doc.txt)
- "Volunteer! If you're busy, you can't spend month and it's a resume booster, too!" (doc.txt)

**Conserving Resources**
- "Turn off the water while brushing your teeth." (doc.txt)
- "Unplug electronics when you aren't using them. Even while turned off, they still use up costly energy." (doc.txt)
