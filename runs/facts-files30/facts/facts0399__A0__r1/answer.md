

Here are the document's instructions as executable bullet points:

**Adventure Screen (Week 1, Day 1)**
- Note the layout: the main window gives a close view around your heroes/cities; the world map (upper-right) shows the whole world, with black areas still unexplored.
- Confirm your first hero, Lord Haart, is selected (he's on his horse, waiting for orders).
- Move the mouse cursor over the town entrance (between the two flag poles); the horse icon rears up to signal the hero will interact with that location, and the location's name/description appears in the Rollover Bar at the bottom of the screen.
- Click the town entrance: green arrows show the path to the large green X (the destination).
- Click the same location again (or simply double-click it) to send the hero; the first click selects the path, the second click moves the hero.
- When the hero reaches the town entrance, the view switches to the Town Screen.

**Town Screen (Castle town type)**
- Observe the town view (built structures appear here) and the bottom-left info: town income per day and troop production per week.
- Note the two rows of boxes right of the town info: top row = troops in the town's garrison; bottom row = troops with a hero visiting the town.
- Build a Fort:
  - Click the Town Hall (the larger of the two buildings on the left) to open the Hall Screen.
  - In the Hall Screen, read the color coding: green bar = buildable now, red bar = buildable later, gray bar = disabled/never buildable, gold = fully built/upgraded.
  - Click the picture of the Fort, review its description and required resources, then click the Build button (lower-left of the popup) to construct it.
- After the Fort is built, click it to open the Castle Window:
  - It lists all seven units the town can produce; built Creature Generators (currently Guardhouse, Archers' Tower, and Barracks) show their Available unit counts.
  - Click Exit (lower right) to return to the Town Screen.
- Buy a spell book:
  - Click the round tower on the right (the Mage Guild), then choose to buy a spell book — Lord Haart is a Knight and starts without one, and no hero can learn or cast spells without a spell book.
  - On the Mage Guild Screen, spells are listed on scrolls on shelves; left- or right-click any scroll to see its description.
  - Click Exit (lower right) to leave.
- Recruit troops (your starting force is only a token force):
  - Click the large central tower (one of three towers along the hill at the back of the town) to open the Recruit Pikemen window.
  - Either use the scroll bar to pick a number (total cost is calculated to the right), or click the Maximum button (four up arrows at the bottom of the popup) to auto-select the most troops you can afford.
  - Click the Recruit button (bottom center); the new Pikemen then appear in the garrison row of your town.
