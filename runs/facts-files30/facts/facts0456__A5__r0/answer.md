<answer>
Your neighbor is only partially right. If your club is in an unincorporated area, it must close at midnight on Saturday and stay closed until 7:00 a.m. Monday [E1]. There is no midnight closing on Sunday.

Relevant rules:
- The chapter applies to unincorporated areas and municipalities without their own ordinances [E3].
- A bottle club is a place where products are sold with the knowledge they will be mixed with or drunk with alcoholic beverages on the premises [E4].
- On-premises sale/service of alcoholic beverages: 7:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m. on all days except Sunday; Sunday only 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m. of the following day [E2].
- Bottle clubs must remain closed 3:00 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily, and from midnight Saturday until 7:00 a.m. Monday [E1].
- Violations are second-degree misdemeanors [E5].
