**1. Neutralization of bovine coronavirus (Clark et al., 1998)**
Theaflavins extracted from black tea were shown to neutralize bovine coronavirus and rotavirus infections. "The combination of theaflavin fractions (TF1 + TF2a + TF2b + TF3) was more active than the sum of the activities of these four fractions individually, indicating synergism amongst the TF components." (doc.txt) The study found that "theaflavin and theaflavin gallate derivatives have inactivation activity (in vitro) against both rotavirus and coronavirus," (doc.txt) and "The crude black tea extract was also able to neutralize the coronavirus." (doc.txt)

**2. Virtual screening against SARS-CoV-2 (Lung et al., 2020, Journal of Medical Virology)**
In the context of the "current pandemic created by the novel corona virus COVID-19," (doc.txt) 83 compounds used in Chinese medicine were screened for binding to the RNA dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) of SARS-CoV-2. The virtual screening "revealed that out of the 83 compounds screened, theaflavin was the best compound on the basis of idock score (prediction of binding affinity), hydrophobic interactions and additional hydrogen bonds between theaflavin and amino acid near the active site of RdRp." (doc.txt) This "was further confirmed by lower binding energy when it docks the catalytic pocket of SARS-CoV-2 RdRp." (doc.txt) The findings "suggested that theaflavins could be used as a lead compound for developing a SARS-CoV-2 inhibitor that targets the RdRp." (doc.txt) The document notes that "Theaflavins are present in black tea and the highest theaflavin contents are present in the black teas of Assam," (doc.txt) and cautions that "further in vivo, animal and clinical trials would be required to carry forward this research finding." (doc.txt)

**3. Inhibition of SARS-CoV 3C-like protease (Chen et al., 2005, Evidence Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine)**
A study from Taiwan "demonstrated the inhibition of SARS-CoV 3C-like protease activity by Theaflavin-3,3'-digallate (TF3)." (doc.txt) The authors "reported that the extracts from Puer and Black tea were more potent than the green or oolong tea extracts in their inhibitory activities against a chymotrypsin-like (3CLPro) protease," (doc.txt) with the target being the 3CL protease and "the virus of interest was SARS- CoV (2002 epidemic)." (doc.txt) Using a docking approach to screen a library of 720 compounds, "Two compounds, tannic acid and TF2b (Theaflavin 3-gallate) were found to be active against 3CL Protease." (doc.txt) The "water extracts of TF2b (theaflavin-3-gallate), TF3 (theaflavin diggallate) and tannic acid were found to be the best effective 3CLPro inhibitors with inhibitory concentration (IC50) of less than 10 μM." (doc.txt)

In short, the document reports in vitro and in silico evidence that black tea's theaflavins (and crude black tea extract) can inactivate/neutralize coronavirus, act as a lead compound for SARS-CoV-2 RdRp inhibitors, and inhibit the SARS-CoV 3C-like protease — with the caveat that in vivo, animal, and clinical trials are still required.
