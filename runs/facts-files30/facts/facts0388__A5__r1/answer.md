<answer>
According to doc.txt, if a meter seal was broken by someone other than SRP's personnel, SRP may assess a reconnection fee to the Customer's billing [E1]. Because the disconnection is treated as a violation of the Rules and Regulations, SRP will not restore service until all amounts due SRP have been paid, and those amounts include the full cost or expense incurred by SRP for the removal and reinstallation of the meter or meters, apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral [E2]. In addition, the Customer's service entrance must comply with SRP's then-current Electric Service Specifications before it can be re-energized [E2].
