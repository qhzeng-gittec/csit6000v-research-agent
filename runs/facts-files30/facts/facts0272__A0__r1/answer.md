

According to doc.txt, the financial industry's adoption of AI/ML has been to solve discrete challenges, such as maximizing profit and minimizing risk. Unlike older algorithms that automated human-coded rules, AI models can "learn" by themselves and make inferences and recommendations not identified by modelers in advance, extending their uses to new financial services and products.

Specific purposes include powering chatbots in customer service functions, identifying investment opportunities and/or executing trades, augmenting lending models or (more sparingly) making lending decisions, and identifying and preventing fraud. The article also notes that financial service providers are likely to continue adopting and promoting AI/ML to save time and money and to promote accessibility, accuracy, and regulatory compliance.

Adoption varies across sectors and firms, reflecting factors such as a firm's ability to fund internal development and regulatory requirements.
