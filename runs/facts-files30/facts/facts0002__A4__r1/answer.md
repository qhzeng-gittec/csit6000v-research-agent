The document "Money Management Tips: 55 Ways to Save Money" [E1] offers the following tips, grouped by category:

**Recreation and Entertainment**
- Don't smoke, since cigarettes are expensive and the money adds up quickly [E2].
- When eating out, look for coupons or special deals, as many restaurants offer them [E3].
- Bring your student ID to the movies, since most theaters give students a discount [E4].

**Food and Basic Needs**
- Ask for generic medications at the pharmacy [E5].
- Don't buy bottled water; instead buy a water filtration pitcher [E6].
- Make home-cooked meals, which are often cheaper than a fast food binge [E7].
- Pack a lunch instead of eating out [E8].

**Clothing**
- Buy clothes at the end of the season when they're on sales [E9].

**Budgeting / Spending Plan**
- Set goals for your spending and saving [E10].
- Keep track of your spending to avoid overspending; there are apps for that [E11].
- Don't use a credit card if it will lead you to make more purchases — on average, people with credit cards spend 34% more [E12].
- Before going out to spend, set a limit for yourself and stick to it [E13].
- Wait at least two hours before making a big purchase to be sure it's something you really need [E14].

**Transportation**
- Keep your tires inflated properly for better gas mileage [E15].
- Carpool with a friend [E16].
- Walk, bike, or ride to school to save on gas [E17].
- Look around for the cheapest gas price before filling up; there are apps for that [E18].

**Savings**
- Only use ATMs of your own bank, since other banks' ATM fees add up [E19].
- Always put part of your paycheck into a savings account [E20].
- Save spare change in a piggy bank or change jar, since it adds up [E21].

**Conserving Resources**
- Unplug electronics when you aren't using them, since they still use up costly energy while turned off [E22].
- Pay your bills online to save paper and money on stamps [E23].
