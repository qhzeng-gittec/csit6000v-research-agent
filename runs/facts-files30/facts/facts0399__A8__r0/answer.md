

Here are the steps from `doc.txt` as executable bullet points:

- **Start on the Adventure Screen.** "What you will see first is the Adventure Screen." (doc.txt) The world map is in the upper-right corner, and unexplored areas are black: "until you send a hero to explore an area you won't know what is there" (doc.txt).
- **Confirm your hero is selected.** "Your first hero, Lord Haart, should already be selected" (doc.txt).
- **Aim at the town entrance.** "place your mouse cursor over the entrance to the town (between the two flag poles)" (doc.txt); the horse icon rearing up means the hero will interact with that location.
- **Click the location to set the path.** "a set of green arrows show the path your hero will take to reach the large green X" (doc.txt), and the X "marks your hero's destination" (doc.txt).
- **Click the same location again (or double-click) to send the hero.** "Click again on the same location and your hero will move to the castle" (doc.txt).
- **Wait for the Town Screen.** "When the hero arrives at the entrance to the town, the view will change to the Town Screen." (doc.txt)
- **Check the town info in the bottom-left corner**, which shows "town income (per day) and troop production (per week)" (doc.txt); the top row of boxes is the garrison, the bottom row is troops visiting with a hero.
- **Build a Fort:** "click on the larger of the two buildings on the left side of the town view – the Town Hall – to enter the Hall Screen" (doc.txt).
- **Read the building color codes on the Hall Screen:** "Any building you can currently build is shown with its name in a green bar" (doc.txt); "Any building that you can not build, but can be built later is shown with its name in a red bar" (doc.txt); "If a building has been disabled or can never be built, its name will appear in a gray bar" (doc.txt); "Once you have completely built or upgraded a structure as high as it will go, its name will be displayed in gold" (doc.txt).
- **Build the Fort:** "Click on the picture of the Fort" (doc.txt) to see its description and required resources, then "Click on the Build button (in the lower left hand corner of the popup window) and the Fort will be constructed." (doc.txt)
- **Inspect the Castle Window:** "click on it to view the Castle Window" (doc.txt), which "shows you information about all seven units that can be produced by this town" (doc.txt); built Creature Generators (currently the Guardhouse, Archers' Tower, and Barracks) show units available. Click Exit (lower right) to return to the Town Screen.
- **Buy a spell book at the Mage Guild** (the round tower on the right): "All heroes must have a spell book before they can learn or cast spells." (doc.txt)
- **Browse spells on the Mage Guild Screen:** "Left or right clicking on any spell scroll will show you a description of that spell." (doc.txt) Click Exit (lower right) to leave.
- **Recruit troops:** "Click on the large central tower. The Recruit Pikemen window is displayed." (doc.txt)
- **Choose the quantity:** use the scroll bar to select a number (total cost is calculated to the right), or "the fastest way to buy troops is to use the Maximum button (the four up arrows at the bottom of the popup window)" (doc.txt), which buys the maximum you can afford.
- **Confirm the purchase:** "Click the Maximum button, then click the Recruit button (bottom center). Notice how there are now Pikemen in the garrison row of your town." (doc.txt)
