<answer>
According to doc.txt, if a customer's meter seal was broken (by someone other than SRP's personnel) and SRP disconnected service, the customer must do the following to get service reconnected:

1. Pay all amounts due to SRP. SRP will not restore service until all amounts due have been paid [E4]. These amounts include:
   - The full cost or expense incurred by SRP for the removal and reinstallation of the meter or meters, apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral [E4].
   - Charges for periods of unmetered service, estimated using data from available records and information; if meters or Service Equipment were damaged, the current Customer of record must pay based on estimated energy usage not previously billed plus any SRP costs associated with restoring proper metering or service [E3].
   - A reconnection fee, which SRP may assess to the Customer's billing when a meter seal has been broken by someone other than SRP's personnel [E1].

2. Ensure the Customer's service entrance complies with SRP's then-current Electric Service Specifications before it can be re-energized [E4].

The document does not describe any additional application or procedural steps beyond paying all amounts due and bringing the service entrance into compliance with the Electric Service Specifications.
