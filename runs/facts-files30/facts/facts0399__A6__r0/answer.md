

**Summary of `doc.txt` (Heroes III tutorial, Week 1, Day 1) as executable steps:**

- **Explore the world:** The world map is mostly black at first — "until you send a hero to explore an area you won’t know what is there" (doc.txt), so send your hero to explore.
- **Move your hero to the town:** "Your first hero, Lord Haart, should already be selected" (doc.txt). To move him, "place your mouse cursor over the entrance to the town (between the two flag poles)" (doc.txt), then double-click — "a fast way to move is to simply double click on an intended destination – the first click selects the path, the second click sends the hero" (doc.txt).
- **Build a Fort:** On the Town Screen, "click on the larger of the two buildings on the left side of the town view – the Town Hall – to enter the Hall Screen" (doc.txt). There, "Click on the picture of the Fort" (doc.txt), check the description and required resources, then "Click on the Build button (in the lower left hand corner of the popup window) and the Fort will be constructed" (doc.txt).
- **Check the Castle Window:** "Now that you have a Fort, click on it to view the Castle Window" (doc.txt), which "shows you information about all seven units that can be produced by this town" (doc.txt). Exit via the Exit button (lower right).
- **Buy a spell book:** "Click on the Mage Guild, then choose to buy a spell book" (doc.txt), since "All heroes must have a spell book before they can learn or cast spells" (doc.txt). On the Mage Guild Screen, left- or right-clicking a spell scroll shows its description; leave via the Exit button (lower right).
- **Recruit troops:** Your starting troops are "really just a token force" (doc.txt), so "Click on the large central tower" (doc.txt) to open the Recruit Pikemen window, then "Click the Maximum button, then click the Recruit button (bottom center)" (doc.txt) to buy as many Pikemen as you can afford and add them to your army.

The document does not cover what to do after these steps (e.g., later days or other town types' details).
