**2. Virtual screening against SARS-CoV-2 (Lung et al., 2020, Journal of Medical Virology)**

- "This virtual screening in this bioinformatics study revealed that out of the 83 compounds screened, theaflavin was the best compound on the basis of idock score (prediction of binding affinity), hydrophobic interactions and additional hydrogen bonds between theaflavin and amino acid near the active site of RdRp." (doc.txt)
- This "was further confirmed by lower binding energy when it docks the catalytic pocket of SARS-CoV-2 RdRp." (doc.txt)
- "These finding suggested that theaflavins could be used as a lead compound for developing a SARS-CoV-2 inhibitor that targets the RdRp." (doc.txt)
- "Theaflavins are present in black tea and the highest theaflavin contents are present in the black teas of Assam." (doc.txt)
- The authors note a caveat: "Though further in vivo, animal and clinical trials would be required to carry forward this research finding..." (doc.txt)

**3. Inhibition of SARS-CoV 3C-like protease (Chen et al., 2005, Evidence Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine)**
