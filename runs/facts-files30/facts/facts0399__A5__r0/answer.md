**Week 1, Day 1 — executable steps** (from doc.txt)

**On the Adventure Screen**
- Note the layout: the main window gives a close view around your heroes and cities; the world map in the upper-right corner shows a small view of the entire world, and most of it is black until you send a hero to explore an area [E2].
- Confirm your first hero, Lord Haart, is selected (he is sitting on his horse, waiting for instructions) [E3].
- Move the mouse cursor over the town entrance (between the two flag poles); the horse icon rearing up on its hind legs means the hero will interact with that location, and the location's name and short description appear in the Rollover Bar at the bottom of the screen [E3].
- Click the location: green arrows show the path to the large green X, which marks the destination; click the same location again to send the hero (fastest: double-click the destination — first click selects the path, second click sends the hero) [E3].
- When the hero arrives at the town entrance, the view changes to the Town Screen [E3].

**On the Town Screen (Castle town type)**
- Understand the screen: the town view shows any structures or upgrades you build; the bottom-left corner shows town income (per day) and troop production (per week); the top row of boxes on the right is the town's garrison troops, and the bottom row is troops currently visiting with a hero [E4].
- Build a Fort: click the Town Hall (the larger of the two buildings on the left side of the town view) to enter the Hall Screen, where all construction decisions are made [E5].
- In the Hall Screen, read the color coding: green bar = building you can currently build; red bar = not buildable now but buildable later; gray bar = disabled or can never be built; gold = completely built or upgraded as high as it will go [E5].
- Click the picture of the Fort to see its description and required resources, then click the Build button (lower-left corner of the popup window) to construct it [E5].
- After the Fort is built, click it to open the Castle Window, which shows information about all seven units the town can produce; already-constructed Creature Generators (currently the Guardhouse, Archers' Tower, and Barracks) show a number of Available units; click Exit (lower right) to return to the Town Screen [E6].
- Buy a spell book: click the round tower on the right (the Mage Guild), then choose to buy a spell book — Lord Haart is a Knight and does not begin with one, and all heroes must have a spell book before they can learn or cast spells [E7].
- On the Mage Guild Screen, spells are listed on scrolls on the shelves; left- or right-click any spell scroll to see its description; click Exit (lower right) to leave [E7].
- Recruit troops: the hero's starting troops are only a token force, so buy available troops and add them to his army [E8].
- Click the large central tower (of the three towers along the hill at the back of the town) to open the Recruit Pikemen window [E8].
- Either use the scroll bar to select a number of troops (total cost is calculated to the right), or, for the fastest purchase, click the Maximum button (the four up arrows at the bottom of the popup) to auto-select the maximum number you can currently afford [E8].
- Click the Recruit button (bottom center); the new Pikemen then appear in the garrison row of your town [E8].
