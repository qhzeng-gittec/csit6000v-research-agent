Based on `doc.txt`, when a meter seal has been broken by someone other than SRP's personnel and service is disconnected for that violation, the customer must do the following before service can be restored:

1. **Pay all amounts due to SRP.** Per Section 4.14.4: "If SRP disconnects service to the Customer because of a violation of these Rules and Regulations, SRP will not restore service to the Customer until all amounts due SRP have been paid." This includes "the full cost or expense incurred by SRP for the removal and reinstallation of the meter or meters, apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral."

2. **Comply with service specifications.** "The Customer's service entrance must comply with SRP's then-current Electric Service Specifications before it can be re-energized."

Additionally, under Section 4.14.1, "When a meter seal has been broken by someone other than SRP's personnel, SRP may assess a reconnection fee to the Customer's billing," and bills for such charges "are due and payable immediately upon presentation unless otherwise agreed by SRP."
