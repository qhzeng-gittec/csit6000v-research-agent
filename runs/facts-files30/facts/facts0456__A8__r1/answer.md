

- Saturday: your neighbor is right — bottle clubs "shall remain closed from midnight on each Saturday until 7:00 a.m. on the following Monday" (doc.txt).
- Sunday: no midnight closing is required; the general rule is that bottle clubs "shall remain closed from the hours of 3:00 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. on each day of the week."
- This applies to "all bottle clubs located in the unincorporated areas of the county."
- On-premises sales: "between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 2:00 a.m. on all days of the week except Sunday"; Sunday: "11:00 a.m. Sunday and 2:00 a.m. of the following day."
- Off-premises sales: 7:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m. on all days except Sunday; Sunday: 9:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m.
- A bottle club is a place where products are sold "with the knowledge... that same will be... mixed... with... an alcoholic beverage, for consumption on the premises."
- Violations are "a misdemeanor of the second degree."
