The document requires closure at Saturday midnight:

- Closing hours: "All bottle clubs located in the unincorporated areas of the county, shall remain closed from the hours of 3:00 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. on each day of the week except that all such bottle clubs shall remain closed from midnight on each Saturday until 7:00 a.m. on the following Monday." (doc.txt)
- Scope: "the unincorporated areas of the county and any municipality which has not adopted an ordinance" (doc.txt).
- Bottle club: a place where products are sold "with the knowledge... that same will be... mixed... with... an alcoholic beverage" (doc.txt).
- General on-premises hours: "between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 2:00 a.m. on all days of the week except Sunday"; Sunday "between the hours of 11:00 a.m. Sunday and 2:00 a.m. of the following day" (doc.txt).
- Violation: "a misdemeanor of the second degree" (doc.txt).
