

According to `doc.txt`, if SRP disconnected service because of a violation of the Rules and Regulations (such as a broken meter seal), the customer must pay all amounts due before service is restored:

- "If SRP disconnects service to the Customer because of a violation of these Rules and Regulations, SRP will not restore service to the Customer until all amounts due SRP have been paid." (doc.txt)
- Those amounts include "the full cost or expense incurred by SRP for the removal and reinstallation of the meter or meters, apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral." (doc.txt)
- Additionally, "The Customer's service entrance must comply with SRP's then-current Electric Service Specifications before it can be re-energized." (doc.txt)

Related to the broken seal itself, the document notes that "When a meter seal has been broken by someone other than SRP's personnel, SRP may assess a reconnection fee to the Customer's billing." (doc.txt)
