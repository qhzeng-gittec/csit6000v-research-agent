

Your neighbor is right about Saturday: "All bottle clubs located in the unincorporated areas of the county, shall remain closed from the hours of 3:00 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. on each day of the week except that all such bottle clubs shall remain closed from midnight on each Saturday until 7:00 a.m. on the following Monday" (doc.txt). No separate Sunday-midnight rule appears.

- "Bottle club shall mean a place or establishment where a product or article, for a consideration, is sold" (doc.txt).
- On-premises: "except between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 2:00 a.m. on all days of the week except Sunday" (doc.txt); "Sunday sales of alcoholic beverages shall only be permitted between the hours of 11:00 a.m. Sunday and 2:00 a.m." (doc.txt).
- "This chapter shall apply to, and be effective within, the unincorporated areas of the county" (doc.txt).
- "Any person violating this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor" (doc.txt).
