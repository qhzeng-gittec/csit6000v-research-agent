Based solely on doc.txt, the research on black tea and coronavirus (the document frames this as antiviral activity rather than "health benefits") covers three main studies:

1. **Clark et al. (1998) – bovine coronavirus:** Theaflavins extracted from black tea were shown to neutralize bovine coronavirus (and rotavirus) infections in vitro. The crude black tea extract and individual theaflavin fractions were tested, and a combination of theaflavin fractions (TF1 + TF2a + TF2b + TF3) was more active than the sum of the individual fractions, indicating synergism among the theaflavin components. The crude black tea extract itself was also able to neutralize the coronavirus [E1].

2. **Lung et al. (2020), Journal of Medical Virology – SARS-CoV-2 (COVID-19):** In the context of the pandemic, 83 compounds from the Chinese medicine system were virtually screened for binding to the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) of SARS-CoV-2, using 3D models of the RdRp of SARS-CoV-2, SARS-CoV, and MERS-CoV. Theaflavin was the best compound based on docking score (predicted binding affinity), hydrophobic interactions, and hydrogen bonds near the RdRp active site, confirmed by lower binding energy when docking the catalytic pocket of SARS-CoV-2 RdRp. This suggested theaflavins could serve as a lead compound for developing a SARS-CoV-2 inhibitor targeting RdRp. Theaflavins are present in black tea, with the highest contents in Assam black teas. The authors note that further in vivo, animal, and clinical trials are required [E2, E3].

3. **Chen et al. (2005), Evidence Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine – SARS-CoV (2002 epidemic):** A study from Taiwan demonstrated that Theaflavin-3,3′-digallate (TF3) inhibits the SARS-CoV 3C-like protease (3CLPro). Puer and black tea extracts were more potent than green or oolong tea extracts against this protease. A docking screen of 720 natural product compounds identified tannic acid and TF2b (theaflavin-3-gallate) as active against 3CL protease, and water extracts of TF2b, TF3, and tannic acid were the most effective 3CLPro inhibitors, with IC50 values below 10 μM [E3, E4].
