

Here is the document summarized as executable bullet points, based only on `doc.txt`:

**1. Start on the Adventure Screen**
- The game opens on the Adventure Screen, "where you will spend a great deal of time while playing Heroes III" (doc.txt). The world map in the "upper-right corner of the screen" shows the whole world, but "most of the world map is black" until a hero explores an area (doc.txt).
- Your first hero, Lord Haart, "should already be selected" (doc.txt).

**2. Move your hero to the town**
- Place the mouse cursor over the town entrance "between the two flag poles"; the horse icon rears up, meaning the hero will interact with that location, and the Rollover Bar at the bottom shows "the name and a short description of the location" (doc.txt).
- Click the location to see the path: "a set of green arrows show the path your hero will take to reach the large green X" (doc.txt).
- "Click again on the same location and your hero will move to the castle" — or double-click the destination, since "the first click selects the path, the second click sends the hero" (doc.txt).
- On arrival, "the view will change to the Town Screen" (doc.txt).

**3. Read the Town Screen**
- This is the Castle town type; "there are eight different town types in Heroes III, each with its own unique creatures and buildings" (doc.txt).
- Bottom-left shows "town income (per day) and troop production (per week)" (doc.txt). The two rows of boxes: "The top row is for any troops that are currently in the town's garrison, the bottom row is for any troops currently visiting a town with a hero" (doc.txt).

**4. Build a Fort**
- Click the Town Hall, "the larger of the two buildings on the left side of the town view," to enter the Hall Screen, "where you make all your construction decisions" (doc.txt).
- Building name colors: green bar = "you can currently build"; red bar = "you can not build, but can be built later"; gray bar = "disabled or can never be built"; gold = "completely built or upgraded a structure as high as it will go" (doc.txt).
- Click the picture of the Fort to see "a description of what the Fort does, as well as what resources are required to build it," then "Click on the Build button (in the lower left hand corner of the popup window)" (doc.txt).

**5. Check the Castle Window**
- Click the Fort to open the Castle Window, which "shows you information about all seven units that can be produced by this town" (doc.txt). Currently "the Guardhouse, Archers' Tower, and Barracks show units available – the other structures have not been built yet" (doc.txt).
- "Click the Exit button (lower right) to return to the Town Screen" (doc.txt).

**6. Buy a spell book at the Mage Guild**
- Click the Mage Guild, "the round tower on the right side of the screen," and "choose to buy a spell book" — "Lord Haart is a Knight, he does not begin with a spell book" and "All heroes must have a spell book before they can learn or cast spells" (doc.txt).
- On the Mage Guild Screen, spells are listed on scrolls; "Left or right clicking on any spell scroll will show you a description of that spell" (doc.txt). Leave via the Exit button (lower right) (doc.txt).

**7. Recruit troops**
- The hero's starting troops are "just a token force, so you should buy any available troops and add them to his army" (doc.txt).
- "Along the hill at the back of your town are three towers. Click on the large central tower" to open the Recruit Pikemen window (doc.txt).
- "Using the scroll bar you can select how many troops you wish to purchase, and the total cost is calculated to the right" (doc.txt).
- Fastest method: use the "Maximum button (the four up arrows at the bottom of the popup window)" to "automatically calculate the maximum number of troops available that you can currently afford," then "Click the Maximum button, then click the Recruit button (bottom center)" (doc.txt).
- Verify success: "there are now Pikemen in the garrison row of your town" (doc.txt).
