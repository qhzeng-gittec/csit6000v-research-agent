

Based on `doc.txt` (Salt River Project Agricultural Improvement and Power District Rules and Regulations), the following applies:

- **Section 4.14.1** states that when a meter seal has been broken by someone other than SRP's personnel, SRP may assess a **reconnection fee** to the Customer's billing, and bills for unmetered electricity may include the full cost or expense incurred by SRP to investigate and confirm diversion of electricity.
- **Section 4.14.4** states that if SRP disconnects service because of a violation of the Rules and Regulations, **SRP will not restore service until all amounts due SRP have been paid**. These amounts include the full cost or expense incurred by SRP for the removal and reinstallation of the meter(s), apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral.
- Additionally, the **Customer's service entrance must comply with SRP's then-current Electric Service Specifications** before it can be re-energized.

So, to get reconnected, the customer must pay all amounts owed to SRP (including the reconnection fee, any charges for unmetered service, and the full cost of removing and reinstalling the meter, apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral), and ensure the service entrance complies with SRP's current Electric Service Specifications before service can be re-energized.
