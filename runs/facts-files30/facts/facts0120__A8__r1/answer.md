**In vitro inactivation of coronavirus (Clark et al., 1998)**
- "it was demonstrated that theaflavins extracted from black tea were able to neutralize bovine coronavirus and rotavirus infections" (doc.txt).
- The crude black tea extract and theaflavin fractions were tested individually and in combination; "The combination of theaflavin fractions (TF1 + TF2a + TF2b + TF3) was more active than the sum of the activities of these four fractions individually, indicating synergism amongst the TF components" (doc.txt).
- "theaflavin and theaflavin gallate derivatives have inactivation activity (in vitro) against both rotavirus and coronavirus" and "The crude black tea extract was also able to neutralize the coronavirus" (doc.txt).

**Virtual screening against SARS-CoV-2 (Lung et al., 2020, Journal of Medical Virology)**
- In the context of the pandemic, "83 compounds used in Chinese medicine system were screened for their potential efficacy against SARS-CoV-2 by assessing their binding efficiency onto this RNA dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) of the COVID-19 virus" (doc.txt).
- "This virtual screening in this bioinformatics study revealed that out of the 83 compounds screened, theaflavin was the best compound on the basis of idock score (prediction of binding affinity), hydrophobic interactions and additional hydrogen bonds between theaflavin and amino acid near the active site of RdRp" (doc.txt).
- "These finding suggested that theaflavins could be used as a lead compound for developing a SARS-CoV-2 inhibitor that targets the RdRp" (doc.txt).
- "Theaflavins are present in black tea and the highest theaflavin contents are present in the black teas of Assam" (doc.txt).
- The authors note that "further in vivo, animal and clinical trials would be required to carry forward this research finding" (doc.txt).

**Inhibition of SARS-CoV 3C-like protease (Chen et al., 2005, Evidence Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine)**
- "the extracts from Puer and Black tea were more potent than the green or oolong tea extracts in their inhibitory activities against a chymotrypsin-like (3CLPro) protease" (doc.txt).
- A docking screen of a "natural product library consisting of 720 compounds" found "Two compounds, tannic acid and TF2b (Theaflavin 3-gallate) were found to be active against 3CL Protease" (doc.txt).
- "The water extracts of TF2b (theaflavin-3-gallate), TF3 (theaflavin diggallate) and tannic acid were found to be the best effective 3CLPro inhibitors with inhibitory concentration (IC50) of less than 10 μM" (doc.txt).

Note: the document reports only in vitro, in silico (docking/virtual screening), and enzyme-inhibition findings; it does not describe any clinical or human health-benefit results for black tea against the coronavirus.
