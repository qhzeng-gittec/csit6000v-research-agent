<answer>
In doc.txt, AWIA Section 2015 is part of the "DWSRF Program Revisions" and concerns the Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF), so all of its subsections relate to water. Briefly:

- **Section 2015(a)**: Amends the SDWA to expressly allow DWSRF funds for projects that replace or rehabilitate aging drinking water treatment, storage, or distribution systems — a category that had not been explicitly identified in statute before and accounts for 66.1% of estimated drinking water infrastructure need [E1].
- **Section 2015(c)**: Revises DWSRF provisions for disadvantaged communities — raises the share of a state's capitalization grant that may be used to subsidize loans from 30% to 35% (with a conditional minimum of 6%) and extends the loan amortization period for disadvantaged communities from 30 to 40 years [E2].
- **Section 2015(d)**: Extends repayment and amortization terms for all DWSRF-financed projects — the initial principal and interest payment is due 18 months after project completion instead of one year, and the amortization period may be extended from 20 to 30 years [E3].
- **Section 2015(e)**: Requires EPA to include the cost of replacing lead service lines in the drinking water infrastructure needs survey (conducted every four years), which EPA uses to allot DWSRF funds among states [E4].
- **Section 2015(f)**: Removes the fiscal-year limitation on the 10% DWSRF set-aside, authorizing states to use a portion of their capitalization grant to fund source water assessments or update existing ones [E6].
- **Section 2015(g)**: Requires EPA to gather information on DWSRF administration from state drinking water administrators and report to Congress on best practices to facilitate applications and improve DWSRF financial management and sustainability [E5].
