

• The authors, "US secretary of state and US secretary of commerce" (doc.txt), liken it to the written word and printing press, quoting Lincoln: "certain inventions and discoveries occurred of peculiar value . . . in facilitating all other inventions and discoveries."

• Systems like GPT-4 are "revolutionising the production of knowledge: vastly increasing the capacity of machines to generate original content, perform complex tasks and solve important problems" (doc.txt) while "dramatically lowering the barriers for people to access" it.

• Hazards include "generating false information, reinforcing bias and discrimination, being misused for repressive or destabilising purposes or proliferating the knowledge to make a bioweapon or conduct a cyber attack" (doc.txt).

• Despite risks, it holds potential "from curing cancer to mitigating the effects of climate change to solving global food insecurity" (doc.txt); "The question is not whether to use it, but how."

• The US, "home to many of the leading companies, technologies and minds driving the" revolution, "has the ability and responsibility to lead on its governance" (doc.txt).

• The US "set out a Blueprint" for a "Bill of Rights with principles for how automated systems are designed and used" and a "Risk Management Framework to help improve user protections" (doc.txt).

• "President Joe Biden announced the next step with a set of commitments from leading companies designed to enhance safety, security and trust" (doc.txt). These will "support new technologies and standards to distinguish between human and" machine-generated content, "encourage companies and individuals to report on systems' capabilities and limitations, and facilitate information sharing," and "promote the development of" systems "designed to address society's greatest challenges."

• The US "looks forward to participating in the UK's Global Summit" and "other opportunities for global engagement to build a more secure future."

• "India will play a critical role" (doc.txt) via the "Global Partnership," and the US is "working on inclusivity" through "discussions with the UN."

• "Today, we're on track to meet just 12 per cent of the UN's Sustainable Development Goals" (doc.txt), which it could change "by accelerating efforts to deliver clean water and sanitation, eliminate poverty, advance public health and further other development goals."

• "No country or company can" shape the future "alone" (doc.txt); success needs "the combined focus, ingenuity and co-operation of the international community."
