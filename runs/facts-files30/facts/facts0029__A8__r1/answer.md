

- The authors — "US secretary of state and US secretary of commerce" — say "we are living through another such invention," comparing this technology to the written word and printing press (doc.txt).
- Generative systems such as GPT-4 are "revolutionising the production of knowledge: vastly increasing the capacity of machines to generate original content, perform complex tasks and solve important problems," lowering access barriers, and hold "an exhilarating potential to improve people's lives," from "curing cancer to mitigating the effects of climate change to solving global food insecurity" (doc.txt).
- Hazards include false information, reinforced bias and discrimination, plus the technology "being misused for repressive or destabilising purposes or proliferating the knowledge to make a bioweapon or conduct a cyber attack" (doc.txt).
- The US, "as home to many of the leading companies, technologies and minds," "has the ability and responsibility to lead on its governance" in partnership with others (doc.txt).
- The US set a Blueprint for a Bill of Rights with "principles for how automated systems are designed and used," and a Risk Management Framework "to help improve user protections" (doc.txt).
- President Joe Biden announced commitments from leading companies to "enhance safety, security and trust," with specific guidelines: mitigating misuse risks; supporting "new technologies and standards to distinguish between human and" machine-generated content; encouraging companies and individuals to "report on systems' capabilities and limitations"; facilitating information sharing; and promoting systems "designed to address society's greatest challenges" (doc.txt).
- Code of conduct: through the G7 and the Japan-led Hiroshima Process, the US wants governance "guided by democratic values," with G7-led action informing "an international code of conduct for private actors and governments, as well as common regulatory principles for states," and aligning domestic approaches in the US-EU Trade and Technology Council (doc.txt).
- On longer-term risks, the US "looks forward to participating in the UK's Global Summit" and other global engagement "to build a more secure future" (doc.txt).
- For developing countries, "India will play a critical role" in the Global Partnership, with inclusivity work "through discussions with the UN" (doc.txt).
- The world is "on track to meet just 12 per cent of the UN's Sustainable Development Goals," and this technology could change that by "accelerating efforts to deliver clean water and sanitation, eliminate poverty, advance public health" (doc.txt).
- "No country or company can shape the future" alone; only "the combined focus, ingenuity and co-operation of the international community" can "fully and safely harness the potential" (doc.txt).
