## Surgical options and their pros/cons

The document lists three main techniques used "to alleviate pressure on the nerve":

**2. Endoscopic spine surgery** — "The surgeon uses a long thin tube, or endoscope, to remove the herniated section of the disc."
- Pros: "The procedure is minimally invasive, requiring a tiny incision. Only a small scar will form, resulting in a quicker recovery" (doc.txt).
- Cons: none specifically listed.

**3. Surgery on the core of the spinal disc** — "The surgeon uses instruments to access the core of the spinal disc then uses a vacuum to remove the core. This makes the spinal disc smaller, which reduces pressure on the nerve."
- Con/limitation: "The surgery is only possible if the outer layer of the disc is not damaged" (doc.txt).

**4. Laminotomy or laminectomy** — "A laminotomy involves the removal of part of the lamina, while a laminectomy is removal of the entire lamina," after which "the surgeon performs a discectomy to remove the herniated disc" (doc.txt).
- Pros: "Lumbar procedures: These help to relieve leg pain or sciatic pain that a herniated disc causes in the lower back region. Cervical procedures: These help to relieve pain in the neck and upper limbs" (doc.txt).
- Con: "Following a laminotomy or laminectomy, a spinal fusion (SF) may be necessary to stabilize the spine" (doc.txt).

**5. Spinal fusion (SF)** — "An SF involves joining two bones together with screws."
- Con: "People who have undergone an SF may experience pain and feel as if the treatment is restricting certain movements" (doc.txt).
- Note: "Typically, lumbar laminotomies require an SF," and cervical laminotomies require an SF if operated from the front of the neck but "rarely require an SF if the surgeon operates from the back of the neck" (doc.txt).

**6. Artificial disc surgery (ADS)** — "the surgeon replaces the damaged disc with an artificial one," as "an alternative to spinal fusion."
- Pros: "A surgeon will usually associate this method with less pain and less restricted movement in comparison to SF procedures" (doc.txt).
- Con/limitation: "Some people who undergo laminotomy may be candidates for artificial disc surgery instead of an SF" (doc.txt) — i.e., not everyone qualifies.
