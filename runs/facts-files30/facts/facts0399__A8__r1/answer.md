

Here are the steps from `doc.txt` as executable bullet points:

**Start: Adventure Screen**
- Expect the Adventure Screen first; "This is where you will spend a great deal of time while playing Heroes III" (doc.txt). The world map in the upper-right shows the whole world, but unexplored areas are black: "until you send a hero to explore an area you won't know what is there" (doc.txt).
- Your first hero, Lord Haart, "should already be selected" (doc.txt).

**Move the hero to the town**
- Place the mouse cursor over the town entrance, "between the two flag poles"; the horse icon rearing up means the hero will interact with that location (doc.txt).
- The location's name and description appear in the Rollover Bar at the bottom of the screen (doc.txt).
- Click the location: "a set of green arrows show the path your hero will take to reach the large green X" (doc.txt).
- Click the same location again to send the hero — "the first click selects the path, the second click sends the hero" (doc.txt); double-clicking is the fast way (doc.txt).
- On arrival at the town entrance, the view changes to the Town Screen (doc.txt).

**On the Town Screen**
- Note this is the Castle town type; "there are eight different town types in Heroes III, each with its own unique creatures and buildings" (doc.txt).
- Check the bottom-left info: "town income (per day) and troop production (per week)" (doc.txt).
- The top row of boxes shows "troops that are currently in the town's garrison"; the bottom row shows "troops currently visiting a town with a hero" (doc.txt).

**Build a Fort**
- Click the Town Hall — "the larger of the two buildings on the left side of the town view" — to enter the Hall Screen (doc.txt).
- In the Hall Screen, building name colors mean: green = buildable now, red = "can be built later", gray = "disabled or can never be built", gold = "completely built or upgraded" (doc.txt).
- Click the picture of the Fort to see its description and required resources, then "Click on the Build button (in the lower left hand corner of the popup window)" (doc.txt).

**Check the Castle Window**
- Click the Fort to open the Castle Window, which "shows you information about all seven units that can be produced by this town" (doc.txt).
- Built Creature Generators show available units — currently "the Guardhouse, Archers' Tower, and Barracks" (doc.txt).
- Click the Exit button (lower right) to return to the Town Screen (doc.txt).

**Buy a spell book at the Mage Guild**
- The round tower on the right is the Mage Guild, "where heroes go to learn the spells that they will cast while adventuring" (doc.txt).
- Click the Mage Guild and buy a spell book: "Lord Haart is a Knight, he does not begin with a spell book" and "All heroes must have a spell book before they can learn or cast spells" (doc.txt).
- On the Mage Guild Screen, spells are "listed on scrolls on the various shelves in the guild"; left- or right-clicking a scroll shows its description (doc.txt).
- Click the Exit button (lower right) to leave (doc.txt).

**Recruit troops**
- The hero starts with only "a token force", so buy available troops (doc.txt).
- Click the large central tower of the three towers along the hill at the back of the town to open the "Recruit Pikemen window" (doc.txt).
- Use the scroll bar to select how many troops to buy; the total cost is calculated to the right (doc.txt).
- Fastest method: "use the Maximum button (the four up arrows at the bottom of the popup window)" to buy the maximum you can currently afford (doc.txt).
- Click the Maximum button, then "click the Recruit button (bottom center)"; the Pikemen then appear in the garrison row (doc.txt).
