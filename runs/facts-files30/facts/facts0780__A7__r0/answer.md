**AT&T**

- Launched its own streaming service: "AT&T has announced plans to introduce a new edge provider—HBO Max—to stream video programming for no extra charge to AT&T customers who are also HBO subscribers; other customers will reportedly be charged a subscription fee." (doc.txt)

**Comcast**
- Gained ownership of an existing edge provider through a content acquisition: "In 2011, Comcast became the majority owner of NBCUniversal, which owns television networks and broadcast stations, and thus obtained minority ownership of Hulu, an edge provider that streams video programming to subscribers." - Planned its own streaming service: "Comcast also announced plans to launch its own video streaming service, Peacock," with three subscription options, including "a free option supported by ads, a premium version with more programming for a fee, and the premium version with no ads for a higher fee," with the premium version "to be offered for free to subscribers of Comcast and Cox Communications."
