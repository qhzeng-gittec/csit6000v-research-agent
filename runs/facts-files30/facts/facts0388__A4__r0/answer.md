<answer>
If a customer's meter seal was broken (by someone other than SRP's personnel) and SRP disconnected service as a result, the customer must do the following to get service reconnected:

1. Pay all amounts due to SRP. Because the disconnection was for a violation of the Rules and Regulations (the broken meter seal), SRP will not restore service until all amounts due SRP have been paid [E3].
2. Pay the full cost or expense incurred by SRP for the removal and reinstallation of the meter or meters, apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral [E3].
3. Pay any reconnection fee that SRP assesses to the customer's billing for the broken meter seal [E1].
4. Ensure the customer's service entrance complies with SRP's then-current Electric Service Specifications before it can be re-energized [E3].

In short: the customer must pay all amounts owed to SRP — including the reconnection fee and the full cost of removing and reinstalling the meter and related equipment — and bring the service entrance into compliance with SRP's current Electric Service Specifications before service can be restored [E1, E3].
