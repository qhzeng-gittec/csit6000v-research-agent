

According to `doc.txt`, if SRP disconnects service because of a violation of the Rules and Regulations (such as a broken meter seal, covered under Section 4.14.1), the customer must do the following before service can be restored:

1. **Pay all amounts due to SRP.** The document states: "If SRP disconnects service to the Customer because of a violation of these Rules and Regulations, SRP will not restore service to the Customer until all amounts due SRP have been paid. SRP will include the full cost or expense incurred by SRP for the removal and reinstallation of the meter or meters, apparatus, wires, and Service Lateral." (doc.txt)

2. **Ensure the service entrance complies with specifications.** "The Customer's service entrance must comply with SRP's then-current Electric Service Specifications before it can be re-energized." (doc.txt)

In addition, the document notes that when a meter seal has been broken by someone other than SRP's personnel, "SRP may assess a reconnection fee to the Customer's billing" (doc.txt), and that "SRP will charge the Customer for periods of unmetered service, estimated using data from available records and information" (doc.txt). If there was damage to meters or Service Equipment, "the current Customer of record shall pay SRP based on estimated Energy usage not previously billed as well as any SRP costs associated with restoring proper metering or service" (doc.txt).
