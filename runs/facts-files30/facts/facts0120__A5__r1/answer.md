Based only on doc.txt, the research on black tea and coronaviruses covers three main studies:

1. **Clark et al. (1998) – in vitro neutralization of coronavirus:** Theaflavins extracted from black tea were able to neutralize bovine coronavirus (and rotavirus) infections [E1]. Theaflavin and theaflavin gallate derivatives showed in vitro inactivation activity against both rotavirus and coronavirus, and the crude black tea extract itself was also able to neutralize the coronavirus [E1]. A combination of theaflavin fractions (TF1 + TF2a + TF2b + TF3) was more active than the sum of the individual fractions, indicating synergism among the theaflavin components [E1].

2. **Lung et al. (2020) – virtual screening against SARS-CoV-2:** In a bioinformatics study published in the Journal of Medical Virology, 83 compounds from the Chinese medicine system were screened for potential efficacy against SARS-CoV-2 by assessing binding to the virus's RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) [E2]. Theaflavin was the best of the 83 compounds based on docking score (predicted binding affinity), hydrophobic interactions, and additional hydrogen bonds near the RdRp active site, confirmed by lower binding energy when docking the catalytic pocket of SARS-CoV-2 RdRp [E3]. The authors suggested theaflavins could serve as a lead compound for developing a SARS-CoV-2 inhibitor targeting RdRp, noting that theaflavins are present in black tea and that the highest theaflavin contents are in the black teas of Assam [E3]. The document stresses that further in vivo, animal, and clinical trials would be required to carry this finding forward [E3].

3. **Chen et al. (2005, Taiwan) – inhibition of SARS-CoV protease:** This study demonstrated inhibition of SARS-CoV (2002 epidemic) 3C-like protease (3CLPro) activity by Theaflavin-3,3′-digallate (TF3) [E3]. Puer and black tea extracts were more potent than green or oolong tea extracts in inhibiting 3CLPro [E4]. Water extracts of TF2b (theaflavin-3-gallate), TF3 (theaflavin digallate), and tannic acid were the most effective 3CLPro inhibitors, with IC50 values of less than 10 μM [E5].
