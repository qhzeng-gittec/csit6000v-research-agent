According to doc.txt, both installment loans and mortgages are repaid with interest and are amortized: the loan is spread out into a series of fixed payments, with each payment including an interest portion and a principal portion. Both can involve a down payment, in which case the amount financed equals the purchase price minus the down payment, and both carry a finance charge.

A mortgage is a long-term loan used specifically to purchase a house, and the lender requires the house to be used as collateral: if the borrower cannot make payments, the lender can take possession of the house. Mortgages generally have longer terms than installment loans, and their terms often change over time—for example, a twenty-year mortgage might be signed for a five-year term, at the end of which a new agreement is required and conditions usually change. Mortgage agreements may also allow lump-sum payments or changes to the payment amount. Amortizing a mortgage involves many more payment periods than other loans. An installment loan, by contrast, is used for major purchases such as vehicles or furniture, with terms from a few years to twenty years or more for real estate.

Choose a mortgage when purchasing a house, since it is designed for that purpose and offers longer terms. Choose an installment loan for major purchases such as vehicles or furniture when the consumer cannot afford the full price up front and does not want to wait years to save.
