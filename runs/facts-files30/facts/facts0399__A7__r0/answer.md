

Here is the document (a Heroes III "Week 1, Day 1" tutorial) summarized into executable bullet points:

**Adventure Screen**
- Note the layout: "The main window provides you with a close view around your heroes and cities, while the world map (located in the upper-right corner of the screen) shows you a small view of the entire world." (doc.txt)
- Expect hidden areas: "until you send a hero to explore an area you won't know what is there." (doc.txt)
- Skip the other buttons for now: "Don't worry about any of the other buttons below the World Map, these will be described in greater detail later." (doc.txt)
- Confirm your hero is selected: "Your first hero, Lord Haart, should already be selected (he's sitting on his horse, waiting for instructions)." (doc.txt)
- Move him to the town: "place your mouse cursor over the entrance to the town (between the two flag poles)" — the horse icon rearing up on its hind legs means the hero will interact with that location, and "the name and a short description of the location appears in the Rollover Bar at the bottom of the screen." (doc.txt)
- Click the location to select the path: "a set of green arrows show the path your hero will take to reach the large green X. It is this X that marks your hero's destination." (doc.txt)
- Send the hero: "Click again on the same location and your hero will move to the castle (a fast way to move is to simply double click on an intended destination – the first click selects the path, the second click sends the hero)." (doc.txt)
- "When the hero arrives at the entrance to the town, the view will change to the Town Screen." (doc.txt)

**Town Screen**
- Check town info: "Specific information about the town is displayed in the bottom-left corner of the screen, including town income (per day) and troop production (per week)." (doc.txt)
- Read the troop boxes: "The top row is for any troops that are currently in the town's garrison, the bottom row is for any troops currently visiting a town with a hero." (doc.txt)
- Build a Fort: "click on the larger of the two buildings on the left side of the town view – the Town Hall – to enter the Hall Screen." (doc.txt)
- In the Hall Screen, read building status: "Any building you can currently build is shown with its name in a green bar. Any building that you can not build, but can be built later is shown with its name in a red bar. If a building has been disabled or can never be built, its name will appear in a gray bar. Once you have completely built or upgraded a structure as high as it will go, its name will be displayed in gold." (doc.txt)
- "Click on the picture of the Fort. You will be shown a description of what the Fort does, as well as what resources are required to build it. Click on the Build button (in the lower left hand corner of the popup window) and the Fort will be constructed." (doc.txt)
- Inspect the Castle Window: "click on it to view the Castle Window. This window shows you information about all seven units that can be produced by this town." (doc.txt) Currently "the Guardhouse, Archers' Tower, and Barracks show units available." (doc.txt) Then "Click the Exit button (lower right) to return to the Town Screen." (doc.txt)
- Buy a spell book: "Click on the Mage Guild, then choose to buy a spell book. Since Lord Haart is a Knight, he does not begin with a spell book. All heroes must have a spell book before they can learn or cast spells." (doc.txt)
- Browse spells: "Each of the spells the Mage Guild offers is listed on scrolls on the various shelves in the guild. Left or right clicking on any spell scroll will show you a description of that spell. When you are ready to leave, click on the Exit button (lower right)." (doc.txt)
- Recruit troops: "Your hero starts with troops, but it's really just a token force, so you should buy any available troops and add them to his army." (doc.txt)
- "Click on the large central tower. The Recruit Pikemen window is displayed." (doc.txt)
- "Using the scroll bar you can select how many troops you wish to purchase, and the total cost is calculated to the right." (doc.txt)
- Fastest method: "the fastest way to buy troops is to use the Maximum button (the four up arrows at the bottom of the popup window) which will automatically calculate the maximum number of troops available that you can currently afford. Click the Maximum button, then click the Recruit button (bottom center)." (doc.txt)
- Verify the result: "Notice how there are now Pikemen in the garrison row of your town." (doc.txt)
