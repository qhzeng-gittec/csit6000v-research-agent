## Types of sippy cups (excluding soft spout, which you already have)

- **Hard spout:** Encourages your child to transition from sucking to tilting and sipping. The document notes it's often best to introduce it after the child has mastered the soft spout — which your daughter has, so this is a natural next step.
- **Straw:** Uses a straw rather than a spout. Some feel a straw is preferable for speech development over a spout, and it helps your child get used to drinking from a straw and using a cup.
- **No spout / flat lid (360 cup):** Spoutless with a flat top; water flows from all edges of the opening to resemble the action of a real cup while still using a lid. They typically lack no-spill valves, and the document says that's a good thing.

For the 6–12 month stage (your daughter's age), the options are spout cups, spoutless cups, and straw cups. The document also advises: handles are helpful at this stage, don't fill the cup to the top so she can maneuver it, and supervise cup use until she's at least 1 year old.
