

Your neighbor is partially correct:

- Bottle clubs in the unincorporated areas of the county "shall remain closed from the hours of 3:00 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. on each day of the week," and "shall remain closed from midnight on each Saturday until 7:00 a.m. on the following Monday" (doc.txt). So you must close at midnight Saturday and cannot reopen until 7 a.m. Monday.
- On other days, including Sunday, you may operate until 3 a.m.
- A "bottle club" is a place where products are sold or served with the knowledge they will be mixed with or drunk in combination with an alcoholic beverage, for on-premises consumption.
- On-premises alcoholic beverage sales: 7 a.m.–2 a.m. on all days except Sunday; Sunday is 11 a.m.–2 a.m. the following day.
- Off-premises sales: 7 a.m.–2 a.m. on all days except Sunday; Sunday is 9 a.m.–2 a.m. the following day.
- Violations are a second-degree misdemeanor.
