**In vitro inactivation of coronavirus (Clark et al., 1998)**
- "it was demonstrated that theaflavins extracted from black tea were able to neutralize bovine coronavirus and rotavirus infections" (doc.txt).
- The crude black tea extract and theaflavin fractions were tested individually and in combination; "The combination of theaflavin fractions (TF1 + TF2a + TF2b + TF3) was more active than the sum of the activities of these four fractions individually, indicating synergism amongst the TF components" (doc.txt).
- "theaflavin and theaflavin gallate derivatives have inactivation activity (in vitro) against both rotavirus and coronavirus" and "The crude black tea extract was also able to neutralize the coronavirus" (doc.txt).

**Virtual screening against SARS-CoV-2 (Lung et al., 2020, Journal of Medical Virology)**
- In the context of the "current pandemic created by the novel corona virus COVID-19," 83 compounds used in Chinese medicine were screened for efficacy against SARS-CoV-2 by assessing binding to its RNA dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) (doc.txt).
- "theaflavin was the best compound on the basis of idock score (prediction of binding affinity), hydrophobic interactions and additional hydrogen bonds between theaflavin and amino acid near the active site of RdRp," confirmed by "lower binding energy when it docks the catalytic pocket of SARS-CoV-2 RdRp" (doc.txt).
- This "suggested that theaflavins could be used as a lead compound for developing a SARS-CoV-2 inhibitor that targets the RdRp," with the note that "Theaflavins are present in black tea and the highest theaflavin contents are present in the black teas of Assam" (doc.txt).
- The authors caution that "further in vivo, animal and clinical trials would be required to carry forward this research finding" (doc.txt).

**Inhibition of SARS-CoV 3C-like protease (Chen et al., 2005, Evidence Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine)**
- "the extracts from Puer and Black tea were more potent than the green or oolong tea extracts in their inhibitory activities against a chymotrypsin-like (3CLPro) protease," with the target being "SARS- CoV (2002 epidemic)" (doc.txt).
- A docking screen of a 720-compound natural product library found "tannic acid and TF2b (Theaflavin 3-gallate) ... active against 3CL Protease" (doc.txt).
- "The water extracts of TF2b (theaflavin-3-gallate), TF3 (theaflavin diggallate) and tannic acid were found to be the best effective 3CLPro inhibitors with inhibitory concentration (IC50) of less than 10 μM" (doc.txt).

Note: the document addresses black tea's *antiviral activity* against coronaviruses (bovine coronavirus, SARS-CoV, and SARS-CoV-2) rather than "health benefits" in a broader sense; it does not discuss other health effects of black tea in relation to the coronavirus.
