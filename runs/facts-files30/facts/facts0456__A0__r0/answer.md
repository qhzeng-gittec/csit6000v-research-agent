Your neighbor is half right: Sec. 3-5 requires bottle clubs in unincorporated areas to close from midnight Saturday until 7:00 a.m. Monday — you must close at midnight on Saturday. On Sunday there is no midnight closing: you may serve until 2:00 a.m. (Sec. 3-3); the club must be closed 3:00–7:00 a.m.

Relevant rules:
- Sec. 3-1: applies to unincorporated county areas and municipalities without their own ordinances; violations are a second-degree misdemeanor.
- Sec. 3-2: a bottle club sells products known to be mixed with alcoholic beverages for on-premises consumption.
- Sec. 3-3 (on-premises): 7:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m. daily; Sunday 11:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m. next day (golf courses, large banquet halls, hotel guest rooms may start 7:00 a.m. Sunday).
- Sec. 3-4 (off-premises): 7:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m.; Sunday 9:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m.
- Sec. 3-5: bottle clubs closed 3:00–7:00 a.m. daily; closed midnight Saturday to 7:00 a.m. Monday.
