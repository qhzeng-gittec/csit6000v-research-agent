<answer>
Executable bullet points from `doc.txt` (Week 1, Day 1 of the Heroes III tutorial):

1. On the Adventure Screen, the world map is in the upper-right corner; most of it is black until you send a hero to explore an area [E1].
2. Make sure your first hero, Lord Haart, is selected (he is sitting on his horse, waiting for instructions) [E2].
3. Place your mouse cursor over the entrance to the town (between the two flag poles); the horse icon rears up, and the location's name and short description appear in the Rollover Bar at the bottom of the screen [E2].
4. Click the location: green arrows show the path to the large green X, which marks your hero's destination [E3].
5. Click the same location again (or simply double-click the destination) to send the hero to the castle [E3].
6. When the hero arrives at the town entrance, the view changes to the Town Screen [E3].
7. On the Town Screen, check the town info in the bottom-left corner (town income per day and troop production per week); the top row of boxes shows troops in the town's garrison, and the bottom row shows troops visiting with a hero [E4].
8. To build a Fort, click the Town Hall (the larger of the two buildings on the left side of the town view) to enter the Hall Screen [E5].
9. In the Hall Screen, read the color bars: green = can build now, red = can be built later, gray = disabled or can never be built, gold = completely built or upgraded [E6].
10. Click the picture of the Fort to see its description and required resources, then click the Build button (lower-left corner of the popup window) to construct it [E7].
11. Click the Fort to open the Castle Window, which shows information about all seven units the town can produce; click the Exit button (lower right) to return to the Town Screen [E8, E9].
12. Click the Mage Guild (the round tower on the right side of the screen) and choose to buy a spell book — Lord Haart is a Knight and does not begin with one, and all heroes must have a spell book before they can learn or cast spells [E10].
13. On the Mage Guild Screen, spells are listed on scrolls on the shelves; left- or right-click any scroll to see its description, then click the Exit button (lower right) to leave [E11].
14. Buy troops: your hero starts with only a token force, so buy available troops and add them to his army [E12].
15. Click the large central tower (one of the three towers along the hill at the back of the town) to open the Recruit Pikemen window [E12].
16. Use the scroll bar to select how many troops to purchase (total cost is calculated to the right), or use the Maximum button (the four up arrows at the bottom of the popup) to buy the maximum number you can currently afford [E13, E14].
17. Click the Recruit button (bottom center); the Pikemen then appear in the garrison row of your town [E14].
