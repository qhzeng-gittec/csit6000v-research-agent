<answer>
Executable bullet points from doc.txt (a Heroes III tutorial for Week 1, Day 1):

- **Move your hero to the town:** With Lord Haart selected, hover the cursor over the town entrance (between the two flag poles) — the horse icon rearing up confirms the hero will interact with that location [E1].
- **Send the hero:** Click the location again, or simply double-click the destination (first click selects the path, second click sends the hero); the view switches to the Town Screen on arrival [E2].
- **Open the Hall Screen:** On the Town Screen, click the Town Hall (the larger of the two buildings on the left side of the town view) to make construction decisions [E3].
- **Build a Fort:** In the Hall Screen, click the picture of the Fort, review its description and required resources, then click the Build button in the lower-left corner of the popup [E4].
- **Check the Castle Window:** Click the newly built Fort to view information on all seven units the town can produce, then click Exit (lower right) to return to the Town Screen [E5].
- **Buy a spell book:** Click the Mage Guild (the round tower on the right side of the screen) and choose to buy a spell book — required because Lord Haart, a Knight, starts without one, and no hero can learn or cast spells without a spell book [E6].
- **Recruit troops:** Your starting troops are only a token force, so click the large central tower along the hill at the back of town to open the Recruit Pikemen window [E7].
- **Max out recruitment:** Click the Maximum button (four up arrows at the bottom of the popup) to auto-select the most troops you can afford, then click the Recruit button (bottom center); the new Pikemen appear in the garrison row [E8].
</answer>
