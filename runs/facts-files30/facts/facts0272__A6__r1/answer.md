

According to doc.txt, AI/ML in finance "has often been adopted in finance to solve discrete challenges, such as maximizing profit and minimizing risk." The article lists concrete purposes: "powering chatbots in customer service functions, identifying investment opportunities and/or executing trades, augmenting lending models or (more sparingly) making lending decisions, and identifying and preventing fraud."

Unlike older algorithms that "automated human-coded rules," new AI models "can 'learn' by themselves and make inferences and recommendations not identified by modelers in advance." The article adds that new data types—alternative, unstructured, and unlabeled data—help extend uses to "new financial services or products."

Also, the article notes that providers adopt AI/ML "to save time and money and promote accessibility, accuracy, and regulatory compliance," while policymakers weigh "the benefits of innovation with existing and potential future challenges and risks."
