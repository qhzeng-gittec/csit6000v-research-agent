

<evidence>
[E1] "All bottle clubs located in the unincorporated areas of the county, shall remain closed from the hours of 3:00 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. on each day of the week except that all such bottle clubs shall remain closed from midnight on each Saturday until 7:00 a.m. on the following Monday." (doc.txt, Sec. 3-5)
[E2] "No alcoholic beverages, of any kind, may be sold, consumed, or served, either by the package or drink, or permitted to be sold, consumed, or served, either by the package or drink, in, at or on the premises of any place holding a license under the division of alcoholic beverages and tobacco of the state department of business and professional regulation, except between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 2:00 a.m. on all days of the week except Sunday. Sunday sales of alcoholic beverages shall only be permitted between the hours of 11:00 a.m. Sunday and 2:00 a.m. of the following day" (doc.txt, Sec. 3-3)
[E3] "This chapter shall apply to, and be effective within, the unincorporated areas of the county and any municipality which has not adopted an ordinance covering the subject matter, activity, or conduct regulated herein." (doc.txt, Sec. 3-1)
[E4] "Any person violating this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree, punishable as provided under state law." (doc.txt, Sec. 3-1)
</evidence>
<answer>
- The rules apply to unincorporated areas and municipalities without their own ordinances; violations are a second-degree misdemeanor [E3, E4].
- Bottle clubs in unincorporated areas must close from 3:00 a.m. to 7:00 a.m. each day [E1].
- Bottle clubs must close from midnight Saturday until 7:00 a.m. Monday — so your neighbor is right about Saturday [E1].
- No midnight closing on Sunday: the club may operate Sunday from 7:00 a.m. to 3:00 a.m. (Monday) [E1].
- On-premises sales of alcoholic beverages are allowed 7:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m. on all days except Sunday; on Sunday, 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 a.m. the following day [E2].
- So your latest Sunday serving time is 2:00 a.m. (Monday) [E1, E2].
</answer>
