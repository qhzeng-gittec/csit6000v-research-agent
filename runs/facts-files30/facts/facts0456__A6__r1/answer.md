

Your neighbor is partially correct. Under Sec. 3-5, bottle clubs in the unincorporated areas of the county "shall remain closed from midnight on each Saturday until 7:00 a.m. on the following Monday" (doc.txt). So you must close at midnight on Saturday; the document provides no exception. On Sunday, the required closure is only 3:00 a.m. to 7:00 a.m.

Relevant rules (doc.txt):
- Scope: applies to unincorporated areas and municipalities without their own ordinance (Sec. 3-1).
- Bottle club: a place where products are sold or served with knowledge they will be mixed with or drunk with an alcoholic beverage on the premises (Sec. 3-2).
- On-premises sales: 7:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m. daily except Sunday; Sunday 11:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m. (Sec. 3-3).
- Bottle clubs: closed 3:00 a.m.–7:00 a.m. daily; closed midnight Saturday–7:00 a.m. Monday (Sec. 3-5).
- Violation: second-degree misdemeanor (Sec. 3-1).
