Here are the executable steps from `doc.txt` (a Heroes III "Week 1, Day 1" tutorial):

**Adventure Screen**
- Note the layout: the main window gives a close view around your hero/cities; the world map (upper-right) shows the whole world, with black areas still unexplored until a hero explores them.
- Confirm your first hero, Lord Haart, is selected.

**Move to the town**
- Hover the cursor over the town entrance (between the two flag poles); the horse icon rears up, indicating the hero will interact with that location, and the Rollover Bar at the bottom shows the location's name/description.
- Click the location to see the green-arrow path to the green X (destination); click the same location again (or simply double-click) to send the hero.
- When the hero arrives, the view switches to the Town Screen.

**Town Screen**
- Check the town info in the bottom-left (income per day, troop production per week); the two rows of boxes show the town garrison (top) and any visiting hero's troops (bottom).

**Build a Fort**
- Click the Town Hall (the larger of the two buildings on the left) to open the Hall Screen, where all construction is done.
- Bar colors: green = buildable now, red = buildable later, gray = disabled/never buildable, gold = fully built/upgraded.
- Click the Fort picture, review its description and required resources, then click the Build button (lower-left of the popup).
- Click the Fort to open the Castle Window, which lists all seven units the town can produce and shows "Available" units from built Creature Generators (currently Guardhouse, Archers' Tower, Barracks); click Exit (lower-right) to return to the Town Screen.

**Buy a spell book**
- Click the Mage Guild (round tower on the right) and purchase a spell book — required before any hero can learn or cast spells (Lord Haart, a Knight, starts without one).
- On the Mage Guild Screen, left- or right-click any spell scroll on the shelves to see its description; click Exit (lower-right) when done.

**Recruit troops**
- Your starting troops are only a token force, so buy available troops: click the large central tower along the hill at the back of town to open the Recruit Pikemen window.
- Either use the scroll bar to pick a quantity (total cost shown on the right) or click the Maximum button (four up arrows) to buy the most you can afford.
- Click the Recruit button (bottom center); the new Pikemen will appear in the garrison row.
