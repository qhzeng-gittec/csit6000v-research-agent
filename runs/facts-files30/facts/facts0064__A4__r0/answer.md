When the offending party is a minor (under 21), the criteria change to a zero-tolerance policy: "any detectable amount of alcohol can result in a DUI charge" [E1].

Regarding felony versus misdemeanor DUI: a first offense DUI in California is a misdemeanor [E2]. A DUI becomes a felony (with enhanced penalties) when it involves "prior DUI convictions, inflicting bodily harm, or other aggravating factors" [E3]. The document notes that, unlike misdemeanor DUI charges, a felony DUI can carry severe consequences, including significant jail time, hefty fines, and a lasting impact on one's civil liberties and future opportunities [E4].
