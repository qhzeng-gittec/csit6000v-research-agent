Your neighbor is only partly right. Under Sec. 3-5, a bottle club in the unincorporated areas must close at midnight on Saturday and stay closed until 7:00 a.m. Monday. But there is **no midnight closing requirement on Sunday** — you may stay open until 3:00 a.m.

Relevant rules (doc.txt):
- **Scope (Sec. 3-1):** Applies to unincorporated county areas and municipalities without their own ordinance; violations are second-degree misdemeanors.
- **Bottle club (Sec. 3-2):** A place where products are sold/served with knowledge they will be mixed with or drunk with alcoholic beverages on the premises.
- **Closing hours (Sec. 3-5):** Bottle clubs closed 3:00 a.m.–7:00 a.m. daily; except closed midnight Saturday until 7:00 a.m. Monday.
- **On-premises alcohol service (Sec. 3-3):** 7:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m. all days except Sunday; Sunday 11:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m. next day.
- **Off-premises sales (Sec. 3-4):** 7:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m. except Sunday; Sunday 9:00 a.m.–2:00 a.m.

So: Saturday midnight closing is required; Sunday it is not.
