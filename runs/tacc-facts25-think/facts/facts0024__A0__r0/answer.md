

Based on `doc.txt`, a digital asset previously sold as a security should be **reevaluated at the time of later offers or sales**. In that reevaluation, there are additional considerations as they relate to the "efforts of others," including but not limited to:

1. **Whether the efforts of an Active Participant (AP), including any successor AP, continue to be important** to the value of an investment in the digital asset.
2. **Whether the network on which the digital asset is to function operates in such a manner** that purchasers would no longer reasonably expect an AP to carry out essential managerial or entrepreneurial efforts.
3. **Whether the efforts of an AP are no longer affecting the enterprise's success.**

This reevaluation fits within the document's broader framework: the analysis focuses on the "reasonable expectation of profits derived from the efforts of others" under the Howey test, examining whether a purchaser reasonably expects to rely on the efforts of an AP (a promoter, sponsor, or other third party) and whether those efforts are the "undeniably significant ones, those essential managerial efforts which affect the failure or success of the enterprise," as opposed to more ministerial efforts. The inquiry is objective, focused on the economic reality of the transaction and the manner in which the digital asset is offered and sold.
