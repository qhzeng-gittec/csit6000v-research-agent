

According to `doc.txt`, the self-employment tax rate is 15.3 percent, split between two components:

- **12.4 percent** is "allocated to the Social Security system" (doc.txt)
- **2.9 percent** is "going to Medicare" (doc.txt)

The document also notes that as a self-employed individual, "you are considered both the employer and the employee, which makes the entire selfemployment tax burden yours" (doc.txt), whereas an employee would have their employer pay half (6.2 percent for Social Security and 1.45 percent for Medicare). You "do, however, get a tax deduction for one-half of the self-employed taxes paid as an abovethe-line deduction to arrive at adjusted gross income" (doc.txt).
