

According to `doc.txt`, the self-employment tax rate is **15.3 percent**, distributed as follows:

- **12.4 percent** is allocated to the **Social Security** system
- **2.9 percent** goes to **Medicare**

The document also notes that as a self-employed individual, you are considered both the employer and the employee, so the entire 15.3% tax burden is yours (compared to an employee, where the employer pays half — 6.2% for Social Security and 1.45% for Medicare). However, you get a tax deduction for one-half of the self-employment taxes paid as an above-the-line deduction to arrive at adjusted gross income.
