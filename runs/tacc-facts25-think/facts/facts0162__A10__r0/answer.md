

Congratulations on your pregnancy. The article in `doc.txt`, titled "Pregnancy nutrition: Foods to avoid during pregnancy," explains the following items that are not acceptable during pregnancy and the reasons why.

**Seafood high in mercury.** The article explains that "some fish and shellfish have levels of mercury that could be harmful. Too much mercury could damage your baby's growing nervous system." It adds that "The bigger and older the fish, the more mercury it's likely to contain." The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) says not to eat the following while pregnant: "Bigeye tuna. King mackerel. Marlin. Orange roughy. Swordfish. Shark. Tilefish."

**Raw, undercooked or tainted seafood.** "To avoid harmful bacteria or viruses in seafood," the article says "Don't eat raw fish and shellfish," with examples including "sushi, sashimi, ceviche and raw oysters, scallops or clams." It also says "Don't eat uncooked seafood that's refrigerated," including "seafood labeled nova style, lox, kippered, smoked or jerky," though "It's OK to eat smoked seafood if it's in a casserole or other cooked dish. Canned and shelf-stable versions also are safe." It advises watching for local fish advisories and cooking seafood well: "Cook fish to an inside temperature of 145 F (63 C)."

**Undercooked meat, poultry or eggs.** "During pregnancy, you're at a higher risk of food poisoning from bacteria. This is called foodborne illness." The article says to "Fully cook all meats and poultry before eating," and to "Cook hot dogs and lunch meats until they're steaming hot. Or don't eat them at all. They can be sources of a rare but serious foodborne illness known as a listeria infection." It also says "Don't eat pates and meat spreads that are stored in a refrigerator," and to "Cook eggs until the egg yolks and whites are firm. Raw eggs can have harmful bacteria." Foods to avoid include "homemade eggnog, raw batter and dough, tiramisu, freshly made or homemade hollandaise sauce, homemade Caesar salad dressing and homemade ice cream." It also says "Don't eat ready-made meat salads or seafood salads. These include ham salad, tuna salad and chicken salad."

**Unpasteurized foods.** "Don't eat or drink anything that contains milk that hasn't gone through a process called pasteurization. Products that have unpasteurized milk could cause foodborne illness." This includes "soft cheeses, such as brie, feta and blue cheese, unless the label says they're pasteurized or made with pasteurized milk," as well as "juice or cider that isn't pasteurized."

**Unwashed fruits and vegetables, and raw sprouts.** "To get rid of harmful bacteria, wash all raw fruits and vegetables well." The article also says "Don't eat any raw sprouts, including alfalfa, clover, radish and mung bean. They might have harmful bacteria. Be sure to fully cook sprouts."

**Too much caffeine.** "Caffeine can cross to the baby, but the effects on the baby aren't clear." Your health care professional might tell you not to have caffeine, or to "limit caffeine to less than 200 milligrams (mg) a day." For reference, "An 8-ounce (240-milliliters, or mL) cup of brewed coffee has about 95 mg of caffeine."

**Herbal tea.** "Not much is known about the effects of certain herbs on fetuses. As a result, don't drink herbal tea unless your health care professional says it's OK. That includes the types of herbal tea made for pregnancy."

**Alcohol.** "No amount of alcohol has been proved safe during pregnancy. To be safe, don't drink any alcohol." The article explains that "Drinking alcohol during pregnancy leads to a higher risk of miscarriage and stillbirth. Drinking alcohol also may result in fetal alcohol syndrome. The syndrome can cause the face to form oddly and cause lower intelligence."

The article also notes that some seafood is safe: "The Dietary Guidelines for Americans advises pregnant people to eat 8 to 12 ounces (224 to 336 grams) of seafood a week," with options including "Anchovies. Black sea bass. Catfish. Cod. Freshwater trout. Herring. Light canned tuna. Oysters. Pollock. Salmon. Sardines. Shad. Shrimp. Sole. Tilapia. Whitefish."
