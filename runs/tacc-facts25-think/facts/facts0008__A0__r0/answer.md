**Key safety points from the document:**
- The safest place for a baby to sleep is on their back, in their own sleep space (cot, crib or moses basket), in the same room as you for at least the first 6 months (Lullaby Trust advice).

- Choose a firm, flat, breathable mattress; ensure no gap between your mattress and the baby's; use feet-to-foot positioning; no pillows, duvets, bumpers, positioners or soft toys.
